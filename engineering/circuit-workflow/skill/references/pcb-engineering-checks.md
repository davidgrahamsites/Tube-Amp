# PCB engineering checks
Original synthesis informed by the pinned zhbi98 PCB source; see integrated-sources.md. Owner: circuit-design agent. Inputs: actual circuit, exact parts, fabrication stack and mechanical/service contract. Output: evidence-backed rule/defect register and verified native edits.

- Preserve functional ownership while reviewing complete inter-board signal, supply and return paths. Place by current loops, noise sensitivity, temperature and service access. Keep supply/high-current loops away from high-impedance inputs; do not split reference paths without analysing their return current.
- Select exact MPN/package before final footprints. Verify pin numbering, polarity, viewing side, body and lead tolerances, courtyards and assembly access against manufacturer drawings. Round plated holes must contain maximum non-round lead cross-section: diagonal sqrt(width_max^2 + thickness_max^2) is a conservative rectangular-lead screen; add documented assembly allowance and check minimum finished hole after tolerance. Oval slots require full lead containment, rounded-end geometry, orientation, plating and finished tolerances. A round pad does not establish a round drill or lead. Include mounting holes and fastener heads/washers/tool approach.
- Check actual bypass dielectric/package and supply-pin-to-capacitor-to-return loop; distance alone is insufficient. Place inductive-load suppression at its load/connector return loop, verify diode polarity/rating and release timing. Do not select solely on nominal capacitance or diode current title.
- Derive trace/via/pad/neck widths from worst-case continuous, peak/startup/fault current, copper thickness, permitted rise, ambient, voltage drop and manufacturer capability. Narrow pad escapes and thermal spokes count. Encode justified rules in native net classes/custom rules and confirm assignment.
- Derive creepage/clearance from actual working/transient voltage, insulation role, material/environment and applicable evidence. Never apply a generic low-voltage gap to tube supplies or mains. Solder mask is not automatically credited as insulation.
- Use the requested 45-degree/arc routing style and review junctions/neck-downs, but do not explain bends as electrons hitting walls. Geometry compliance does not prove EMI/RFI performance. Review loop areas, decoupling, chassis/shield/earth connections, coupling paths and harness routing; validate physical noise later.
- Keep fixed cabinet mounts independent of removable panels. Reserve complete cable, tube/socket removal, thermal and tool access envelopes. A board-outline area comparison or attractive render cannot prove assembled fit.
- Keep rule exclusions visible. Report DRC, remaining unconnected items, source parity, unsupported footprints, current/insulation qualification and physical checks separately. Export the fabricator's agreed drill/slot package, then inspect CAM geometry/origins/plating. No generic automatic layer upgrade or ground pour without return-path analysis.
