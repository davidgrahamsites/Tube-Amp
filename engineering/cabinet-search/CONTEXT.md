# cabinet-search contract

Owner: Renegade integration agent. One job: retain cabinet search, quote and correspondence history.

## Inputs
Paths in this contract are project-root-relative unless named as local. Read `brain/records/CMP-001-mable-5e3-shell.md` and `engineering/whole-amp/mechanical/fit-register.json`. Local edit/evidence surfaces include `2026-11-12.md`, `mable-dimensions-followup.txt`.

## Process
Historical sourcing. Cabinet and tank are delivered; do not reopen cabinet procurement from these older drafts. Keep source inputs, stable scripts and run products identifiable by their registered roles. This contract adds navigation in place; it does not rerun, relocate or approve the existing design. New active runs belong in a revision module with separate tools, evidence and products.

## Outputs
The local files listed above and their children remain the module outputs. `brain/catalog/FILES.json` indexes their roles and hashes; `brain/materials/register.json` identifies the owning material route.

## Human check
Open the local output with its source/reference and confirm revision, unresolved limits and actual part or circuit identity before using it for the build. For native CAD, inspect the render and pin/geometry checks; for runs, inspect assumptions and logs.
