# Whole amplifier engineering

One job: integrate the original six-tube amplifier, supplies, controls and solid-state spring reverb into the delivered cabinet.
Inputs: ../../Renegade_Codex_Handoff.md, board-plan.json, component-selection evidence and mechanical/fit-register.json.
Process: preserve routed v0.5 drafts; use mechanical for the fixed cabinet envelope, expression-control for pedal requirements, and reverb-600 for the received tank circuit revision. Historical tools are guarded against overwriting native edits.
Outputs: integrated native schematics, PCB drafts, simulations, mechanical evidence and release work orders.
Human check: inspect selected parts and a complete populated assembly against the actual cabinet before fabrication; verify electrical and thermal behavior on the bench before use.
