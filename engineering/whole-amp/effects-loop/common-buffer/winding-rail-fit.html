Vocabulary key

Raw trough
Lowest voltage on the unregulated supply during a ripple cycle.
Self-resonant frequency
Frequency where the winding inductance resonates with its own capacitance.
Thermal resistance
Temperature rise per watt, in kelvins per watt.
Junction
The active silicon inside the regulator package.
Guard
The protective cover around the external heatsink fins.
