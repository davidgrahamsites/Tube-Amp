Sudden rail loss at the effects return

CB-008 · 28 November 2026 · Hold three-board placement. A normally conducting shunt caught the modeled rail-loss transient before it reached 1 V at a silent mixer. This requires a short signal path with controlled feedthrough and a measured fast turn-on. Select the Triad VPT30-1670 for the auxiliary supply trial. Vocabulary key

Feedthrough
Unwanted coupling that lets a fast voltage change bypass the delay network.
Hold-up
Time stored charge can power the trip circuit after its feed disappears.
Headroom
Voltage left between a regulator input and its required output.
RMS current
Effective winding current used to estimate heating.
THD
Harmonic distortion relative to the fundamental signal.
