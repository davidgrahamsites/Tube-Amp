Effects-return mute devices

The effects return drives the mixer through the two delay windings. A normally-on pair of transistors shorts the mixer input during a fault. In PLAY, the gates hold both transistors off. Charge stored in an open transistor can change the treble response. Releasing its gate can inject an audible pulse. The quiet-signal gain limit is ±0.50 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz. The switching-pulse limit is 0.70 V. Previous network and cabinet fit kit.

Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

AC
Alternating current; current that changes direction.
Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Capacitance
The ratio of stored charge to voltage; measured in farads (F).
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Clearance
The shortest distance through air between conductive parts.
Feedback
A signal returned from an output to an earlier stage to control gain or response.
Ground
A named circuit reference; it is not automatically protective earth.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
Impedance
Opposition to alternating current, including resistance and frequency-dependent effects; measured in ohms (Ω).
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Loop resistance
The total resistance of the supply and return conductors, contacts and terminations.
Nominal
The stated reference value before tolerance and operating variation.
Qualification
Checks that show a design meets stated limits under stated conditions.
Reservoir
A capacitor bank that stores energy after rectification.
Tolerance
The permitted variation from a specified value.
Winding
A coil of insulated wire in a transformer or inductor.
Gate
A checkpoint that requires specified evidence before the next activity.
Thermal
Related to heat or temperature.
Cathode
The tube electrode that emits electrons when heated.
Divider
Series components that produce a fraction of an applied voltage.
THD
Total harmonic distortion; harmonic content relative to the fundamental, using the stated harmonic range.
Corner
A selected combination of component and operating limits.
