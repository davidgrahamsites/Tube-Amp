Hold the three-board layout. The next discriminating electrical check is the actual opposed MOSFET pair on the intended driver. Measure effective terminal capacitance and mixer gain at low level and 135 V peak, then measure the mute pulse on the same assembly. The model has separate uncertainty in the low-voltage capacitance, gate charge and loop inductance. Use the film reservoir at the PA pins for the protection fixture. Vocabulary key

Shunt
A device that mutes the signal by connecting it to the return conductor.
Reservoir
A capacitor that stores energy on a supply rail.
Case-to-air thermal resistance
The temperature rise of the package case for each watt dissipated, for the installed cooling path.
Cutoff
The independent circuit that disconnects power when the protected supply rises too far.
Operating point
Steady circuit voltages and currents used as the start of a simulation.
Junction
The active silicon inside a regulator or amplifier package.
Gain error
The output level relative to its target, expressed in decibels.
Precharge bypass
The switch that takes a startup resistor out of the power path after charging.
