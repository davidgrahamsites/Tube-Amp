# results
Owner: electric integration.
Inputs: ../tools and current circuit. Process: generated by run.py, send_isolation.py, solver_review.py, send_driver.py and audit.py, overwritten in place on rerun. Outputs: decks, logs, summary and selected waves. Human check: inspect scope and completion for every case; these are model results, not bench evidence.

Deck export strips trailing whitespace without changing circuit tokens. Log export normalizes carriage returns and trailing spaces; simulator messages and values are retained. No waveform qualification is inferred from logs alone.
