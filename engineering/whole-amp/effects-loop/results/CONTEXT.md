# results
Owner: electric integration.
Inputs: ../tools and current circuit. Process: generated by run.py, send_isolation.py, solver_review.py, send_driver.py, return_driver.py and audit.py, overwritten in place on rerun. Outputs: decks, logs, summary and selected waves. Human check: inspect scope and completion for every case; these are model results, not bench evidence.

Deck export strips trailing whitespace without changing circuit tokens. Log export normalizes carriage returns and trailing spaces; simulator messages and values are retained. No waveform qualification is inferred from logs alone.

RETURN source model is fetched from the official TI ZIP and SHA-256 pinned in return_driver.py; it is not redistributed here. Initial failure evidence is retained separately from rerun cases. A successful operating point or completed transient alone is not headroom acceptance or integrated-loop qualification.

Power-target products are generated by ../tools/power_target.py (base, --refine, --target-check) and audited by ../tools/audit_power_target.py. Read ../power-target-review.md before using the numbers; a passed evidence audit does not qualify the ideal transformer.

magnetics-* retains the initial coupled-model reference and failures. protection-screen/target/compare/stress/corners/retry are exploratory products (summary factory hashes describe files at summary completion). protection-acceptance-* is the frozen final matrix with per-worker source/factory/deck hashes. The audit distinguishes finite numerical acceptance from actual tube/transformer/thermal qualification. Timeout limits refer to wall time, not shortened electrical test windows.

feedback-* products are generated by ../tools/feedback_*.py, audit_feedback.py and plot_feedback.py. The283-check evidence audit explicitly includes rejected compensation and a coarse-timestep false negative. Read ../feedback-review.md before interpreting completion or curves as stability evidence.

feedback-treble-comparison.csv and feedback-treble-summary.json record FB-113 matched gain changes across Presence and transformer assumptions. Source: ../tools/summarize_treble.py; interpretation: ../feedback-review.md.

loaded-* products come from ../tools/loaded_feedback_model.py, loop_probe.py and their dedicated checks/sweep/audit/plot factories. Loaded-run logs retain the adapter messages verbatim (including carriage returns), unlike earlier normalized run logs. The corrected burst metric subtracts operating-point DC; raw peak and correction explanation are retained. The 312-check audit includes expected failures and does not imply that all 87 AC cases pass the response screen. Read ../loaded-feedback-review.md.
