FIT-012 · 27 November 2026 · Partial geometry check

Board screw access

Two power-board screw positions fail the proposed drill-clearance allowance. The measured 28 mm bit projection leaves the chuck beside the tall C26 and C25 capacitor bounds. Keep these positions open for a mounting-layout change. The result does not prove that the actual tapered chuck contacts a capacitor.

Assembly condition

Remove the wooden back, withdraw the carrier, and place the board horizontally before this approach. This study assumes that condition; it does not prove carrier withdrawal or cable disconnection. The wooden back carries no chassis mounting flange. The current four-board draft remains unchanged; this is not the final three-board assembly.

Power-board view

Measured drill and proposed allowance

The reported tip-to-rear length is 210 mm. Bit projection is 28 mm. Maximum chuck diameter is 46.5 mm. Motor width is 51 mm. Chuck length, taper, housing height and housing offset from the shaft remain unknown. The model extends the maximum chuck cylinder from its front to the tool rear; it does not represent the complete motor housing.

\[r_{\rm screen}=\frac{46.5}{2}+2=25.25\;\mathrm{mm}\]

The 2 mm handling allowance is a design proposal, not a measured tolerance. The capacitor contract gives a maximum body height of 41 mm. Add 1 mm for assumed seating height and 2 mm for axial handling clearance.

\[L_{\rm exposed,min}=41+1+2=44\;\mathrm{mm}\]

A 44 mm exposed-bit trial clears the known heights only. It increases the modeled tip-to-rear bound from 210 to 226 mm if the same drill is used. Check the shaft, holder and rear housing separately. A nominal 45 mm bit is partly inside its holder and does not establish 44 mm exposed reach.

Only 31 package bounds are modeled. The remaining 231 non-mounting footprints have no qualified body bound in this check. The four large film capacitors are unselected Panasonic fallback parts. Their presence in a native footprint does not select them for purchase.

Ten arithmetic checks pass. FreeCAD independently checks 372 tool/body pairs across three tool cases; all 381 CAD and export checks pass. Passing means that the two methods agree, including the two flagged approaches. It does not mean that all screws are accessible. The STEP export contains four drilled board solids and 31 body-bound solids. Board display positions are separated by 400 mm; they are not cabinet locations.

Optional assembly tools

The Xiaomi MJDDLSD003QW candidate has a published 16.4 mm body diameter and 159 mm body length. The user's pictures state 28 mm and 45 mm overall bit lengths; they do not state exposed reach. The Taobao screenshot selects the electric version and shows CNY 149.60. Seller identity, exact supplied model and checkout price are unverified. No purchase or tool substitution was approved.

The optional precision-driver cylinder clears the 31 known bounds. It is a conservative 204 mm length bound formed by adding the body length and full long-bit length, not a measured assembled dimension. It does not qualify fastener torque, unknown parts or hand clearance.

The user also requested a rechargeable right-angle option. Bosch IXO 7 with angle attachment 1600A001Y8 is a researched candidate. The attachment changes the direction of the bit. Its assembled tip-to-head-back distance and head width are not verified, so no right-angle clearance result is claimed. A folding handle alone does not establish a right-angle drive. Neither optional tool changes the required service-access design.

Vocabulary key

Body bound
A simple solid that contains the specified maximum part body.
Bit projection
The exposed distance from the chuck front to the bit tip.
Radial allowance
Extra space around the tool axis for handling.
Axial allowance
Extra space along the tool axis.
STEP
A CAD exchange file containing three-dimensional geometry.
Carrier
The removable metal support for the assembly.

Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

Chassis
The metal structure that supports and encloses the electronics.
Chuck
The drill part that grips the bit.
Clearance
The shortest distance through air between conductive parts.
Flange
A projecting edge used to attach or stiffen a part.
Nominal
The stated reference value before tolerance and operating variation.
Tolerance
The permitted variation from a specified value.
