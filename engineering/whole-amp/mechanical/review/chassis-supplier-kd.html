Custom fabrication scope

  • Upper removable carrier and control panel, with mounts independent of the wooden back.
  • Lower power enclosure for the transformer, rectifier and capacitor assembly, if the final arrangement retains that proposal.
  • Internal partitions, cable restraint points and separate protective-earth fixing.
  • Exact holes and slots from the selected sockets, connectors, controls and fasteners.
  • Clear screw approaches, board supports and removal paths.

These are our requirements, not confirmed supplier capabilities. Keep the audio, power and reverb functional blocks intact. Decorative finishes remain separate from electrical bonding and mounting surfaces.

Information needed before quotation or release

  1. Finish the populated assembly and fastener layout.
  2. Issue dimensioned folded-part drawings and STEP/DXF files.
  3. Ask for a one-piece prototype price and a separate five-piece price.
  4. Confirm alloy, sheet thickness, bend radius, hole tolerance, finish and deburring.
  5. Confirm whether the supplier can add captive fasteners and preserve specified bare bonding areas.
  6. Check the supplier drawing against the cabinet and actual hardware before manufacture.
