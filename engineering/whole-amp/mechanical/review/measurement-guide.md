# Cabinet measurement card

Owner: mechanical integration. Status: A-front, A-rear, B, C1 and C2 reported by user; see ../fit-register.json for values and datum limits. Requested spot measurements complete; full assembly fit remains open. Inputs: delivered cabinet and manufacturer drawings in ../fit-register.json. Output: user-reported dimensions to reconcile into that register, with datum and uncertainty. Do not replace unknowns with drawing values or estimates.

## Set up
Stand the cabinet upright and face its open back. Use a retractable steel tape with millimetre markings. Keep it straight; a cloth sewing tape can bend and stretch. Read each measurement twice. Aim for agreement within1mm for this first fit check. No giant calipers or volume measurement is needed.

## A — Clear inside width, left to right
1. Find the underside of the wooden top inside the cabinet.
2. Measure50mm down from that surface. Put a small masking-tape mark on each inside side wall at this height. This is a repeatable inspection height, not a final chassis-height requirement.
3. Near the front, stretch the tape horizontally between the two side walls at the marks. If a support projects into the space, use the exposed face of that support as the limit. Record **A-front**.
4. Repeat near the rear edge at the same height. Record **A-rear**.
5. If an obstruction makes a narrower gap between those locations, record its width and location too. We need usable empty space for the chassis.

## B — Depth, front to back
1. Stay at the same50mm-below-roof height.
2. Find the wooden board the speaker mounts on. Its surface facing the open back is the starting surface.
3. Hold the tape horizontally, straight toward the open back, from that surface to the rear edge of the cabinet. Record **B-rear-edge** and label it “rear edge”. A straight piece of wood held across the rear edges can make the endpoint easier to see.
4. If a rear panel, rail or support projects into this space, also measure from the same speaker-board surface to the nearest face of that obstruction. Record **B-obstruction** and name the obstruction.
5. Record left and right separately if the obstructions differ. Keep the tape square to the speaker board, rather than running diagonally to a convenient corner.

## C — Opposite speaker mounting holes
1. Find the four holes or bolts intended to hold the speaker.
2. From your view through the back, measure centre-to-centre from **top-left to bottom-right**. Record **C1**.
3. Measure centre-to-centre from **top-right to bottom-left**. Record **C2**.
4. Use the middle of each hole or bolt, not its near edge. These are the two opposite pairs, not adjacent holes. If hidden, leave pending and describe what prevents access; do not guess or dismantle the speaker for this request.

These spot checks resolve the immediate selection questions. They do not certify final fit of transformers, hot tubes, speaker magnet, tank, boards or service clearances. Tank body/mount dimensions and a complete assembly check remain separate tasks.
