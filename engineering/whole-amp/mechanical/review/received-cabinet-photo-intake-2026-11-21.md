## Mounting proposal
Develop a folded metal control panel/chassis with mounting flanges or angle brackets secured to solid cabinet structure. Pots transfer torque into metal, not PCB joints. Flange describes attachment; recessed or flush describes the face elevation. These choices are not mutually exclusive.

Check the folded-metal thickness, rear edge and assembly insertion path against the upper rear cover. Keep that cover independently removable. Do not attach any chassis or control-panel mount to the removable rear cover, including secondary or shared supports. User correction FB-070 makes this a firm requirement. Keep its screws accessible and its removal path clear without releasing chassis/control-panel mounts. Flange size, fasteners, exact control-face elevation and mounting holes remain unknown. Existing panel-study drawings remain plan-envelope studies, not fitted chassis or drill templates.

## Tube visibility requirement and priority
Try to expose natural tube heater glow through the two marked oval slots. Preserve the existing compartmentalized functional blocks. Evaluate placement and orientation of complete blocks, then sightlines; do not scatter tubes or lengthen sensitive wiring just to fill both windows. One tube per slot and visibility of all six tubes are not requirements.

Keep socket terminals and high-voltage wiring behind protective metalwork. Assess a ventilated guard that permits a view while preventing contact with hot glass and hazardous parts. Guard geometry, earthing where applicable, ventilation, tube-to-wood spacing and reach protection require engineering verification. Do not cut shielding simply for visibility. Retain socket access, tube removal space and speaker/transformer clearances.

Visible glow is not yet demonstrated and depends on selected tube construction, shields, viewing angle and ambient light. No heater-voltage or bias change is authorized for brightness. No added decorative lighting is selected.
