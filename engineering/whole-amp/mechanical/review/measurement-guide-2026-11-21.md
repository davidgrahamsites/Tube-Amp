Use a rigid ruler or steel tape with millimetre marks. Keep it straight. Read to the nearest millimetre; do not report more precision than you can see. Put the ruler's zero mark at the stated start surface. If zero cannot reach it, use the 10 mm mark and subtract 10 mm from the far reading. Cabinet upright, speaker grille facing front, open back behind it. For P1 and P2-clear, leave the upper rear wooden cover screwed ON in its normal position. For P3, remove it only if needed to reach the fixed inside surfaces. No drilling is required.

1. **P1 — top control-opening width:** look straight down at the cutout behind the carrying handle. Measure horizontally from its left inside wooden edge to its right inside wooden edge, across the narrow straight section. Do not measure the full cabinet width. Record changes in width or ledges rather than assuming a rectangle.
2. **P2-clear — actual clear opening depth, back ON:** at the middle of the opening, put the zero mark against the **cut wooden edge nearest the carrying handle**. Run the ruler straight toward the back. Read where it reaches the **inside edge of the top of the rear wooden cover**, the edge facing into the cabinet. Measure the empty gap only. Do not include the cover thickness or measure to its outside face. This replaces the previous P2-to-outside-edge instruction. The older illustration's dashed P2 endpoint is superseded by this written instruction.
3. **P3 — inside height:** you confirmed that there is no obstruction. Look through the open back. Put the ruler vertically through the middle. Measure from the **underside of the top wood** down to the **top surface of the inside floor**. Both endpoints are inside the cabinet: exclude the thickness of the top and bottom wood. Take one centre measurement and record “no obstruction; floor”. Do not measure diagonally. This dimension is a screening input, not proof that installed tubes, transformers and boards will clear each other.
4. **S1 — speaker aperture, no calipers needed:** from the open back, hold a ruler or steel tape horizontally across the large round hole, flat against the wooden baffle. Slide it slowly up and down while keeping it horizontal. Record the largest inside-edge-to-inside-edge reading. Repeat with the ruler vertical, sliding left and right. Record both readings, to the nearest1–2mm for this preliminary screen. Do not use the small bolt holes or press on the grille cloth. Exact centre alignment is unnecessary at this accuracy: for an illustrative270mm circle, a line10mm off-centre measures269.258mm, only0.742mm short. This is geometry illustration, not the actual hole diameter. Final speaker selection needs its full-size template or trial fit; a rough aperture reading does not grant a machining/fit release.
5. **T1 — tank length:** disconnect cables. Put the tank on its feet. Measure its greatest end-to-end length, including the mounting flanges.
6. **T2 — tank width:** measure its greatest front-to-back extent, from the far back edge to the tips of the RCA sockets. Include fixed projections; exclude loose cables. Two straight objects placed against the extreme edges can transfer this distance onto the ruler.
7. **T3 — tank height:** with the tank resting normally on its feet, measure from the tabletop to its highest top surface. A straight ruler laid lightly across the top helps transfer this height to a vertical ruler. Do not compress the feet.
