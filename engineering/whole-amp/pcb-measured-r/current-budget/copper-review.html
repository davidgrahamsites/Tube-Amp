Size width and copper thickness together

The JR4 candidate now uses 0.6 mm tracks on both complete wet-control nets. The new ground branch also uses 0.6 mm. Native DRC reports zero violations and zero opens. This is a scoped correction; the remaining power, heater and return paths are not qualified.

Specify finished copper thickness, not just board thickness or copper weight. Base foil and finished copper differ after processing. The published JLCPCB barrel value is an average of 18 µm, so it does not establish the requested 20 µm minimum. Eurocircuits thickness explanation; JLCPCB capabilities, accessed 26 November 2026.

Do not substitute 2 oz copper without reviewing the footprints. JLCPCB lists a 0.254 mm annular-ring requirement for 2 oz; the proposed JR4 footprint has a nominal 0.24 mm ring. Thicker copper changes the fabrication constraints as well as resistance. No 2 oz upgrade is approved.

Load and loss calculation

The source still specifies a 100 kΩ reverb pot and 1 MΩ wiper shunt. The retained tolerance screen uses 80 kΩ and 980 kΩ. For a forced 18 V peak excitation, placing both loads directly across the source overbounds their passive load:

\[I_{\mathrm{load}}\le\frac{18}{80000}+\frac{18}{980000}=0.243367\,\mathrm{mA}\]

The 0.25 mA screen leaves 6.633 µA for normal input loading. Confirm the selected op-amp's full-temperature input-current limit before closing that allowance. This excitation is a diagnostic bound; it does not predict available output swing or cover a short, plug transient or startup.

\[w_{\min}=0.8w_{\mathrm{drawn}},\qquad A_{\min}=w_{\min}t_{\min}\]

\[\rho(T)=1.724\times10^{-8}[1+0.00393(T-20)]\;\Omega\,\mathrm m\]

\[R=\frac{\rho L}{A},\qquad V_{\mathrm{drop}}=IR,\qquad P=I^2R\]

At 0.6 mm drawn width and 30 µm finished thickness, the screened cross-section is 0.0144 mm². Doubling width or thickness halves DC resistance at the same length and temperature; it does not automatically double the safe current. NBS copper data supports the material approximation; it is not a guarantee for selected foil.

For each via, use a conservative 0.32 mm finished bore, the requested 0.020 mm barrel and 1.76 mm board length:

\[A_{\mathrm{barrel}}=\pi[(d/2+t)^2-(d/2)^2],\qquad R_{\mathrm{via}}=\frac{\rho L_{\mathrm{board}}}{A_{\mathrm{barrel}}}\]

Temperature and remaining paths

70°C is an assumed copper temperature for resistance, not a calculated thermal rise. Brooks and Adam's study distinguishes conductor resistance from the board's heat flow and shows why a simple area calculation cannot certify trace temperature. No IPC-2152 thermal approval follows from this screen. Brooks and Adam, Trace Currents and Temperatures Revisited, 2015 (author paper, mirrored copy); author archive.

Include pad necks, thermal spokes, vias, connectors and wires; retain high-voltage spacing. Finish these checks before promoting the candidate or issuing fabrication files.
