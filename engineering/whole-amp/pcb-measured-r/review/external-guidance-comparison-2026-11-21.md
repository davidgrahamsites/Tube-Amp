# External layout guidance compared with the electric build

Owner: PCB integration. Date: 21 November 2026. Input: user-supplied repository, manufacturer guidance and the current four-board audit. Output: applicability decisions and repair priorities. Human check: open the cited device documents beside the actual layout and inspect complete current loops and assembled clearances. This report does not release boards for fabrication.

## Conclusion

Use the repository as a review aid. Retain Main, Power, Control and Reverb as functional blocks. The existing design already has most of the right written requirements; the native implementation has not yet met them. The highest-value corrections are complete circuit/part interfaces, local bypass and feedback loops, relay suppression, current/insulation rules, and populated cabinet fit. Corner shape is a separate, bounded requirement.

The external repository was inspected at [commit f316a68016443b4a43d8813d4ab1062eeff29243](https://github.com/zhbi98/pcb-layout-design/tree/f316a68016443b4a43d8813d4ab1062eeff29243). Reviewed its SKILL and references for mechanics, stackup/returns, geometry/coupling, manufacturing/KiCad, signal interfaces and review checklists. Its useful organizing principle is to distinguish hard electrical/mechanical/manufacturing constraints from routing preferences, then check the actual evidence. It is not an amplifier safety standard or a validated automatic router. No external installer, script or instruction was executed. The K230-specific case study was excluded from this amplifier assessment.

## Primary-source checks and limits

**Corners and fast behavior.** [TI SCAA082A, High-Speed Layout Guidelines, §2.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/scaa082a/scaa082a.pdf) recommends avoiding right-angle traces in its high-speed context. That supports a good routing convention; it does not demonstrate audible distortion from a particular audio trace corner. Electrons do not need extra time to steer around a wall. Our acceptance basis for 45-degree/arc geometry remains the user's explicit requirement. Loop area, return impedance and sensitive-node coupling deserve higher electrical priority.

**Audio still needs local layout control.** [TI OPA2134 datasheet, supply/layout sections](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/opa2134.pdf) supports local supply bypassing and compact feedback. Its approximately 8 MHz gain-bandwidth product is a reminder that the reverb amplifier can respond above the audible band. Audio bandwidth alone does not clear stability or RF pickup. [TI SN74HC14](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc14.pdf) separately supports nearby supply decoupling for our logic candidate. Each device must be checked in its actual package and circuit.

**New concrete discrepancy.** [TI TPS3808, §8.4.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf) specifies a nearby 0.1 µF ceramic bypass and a low-impedance supply connection. Coverage currently assigns C353 the KNSCHA MEB104J63V82CB0236 film part. The value matches but the dielectric does not match that guidance. U3's exact ordering code is still open. Therefore neither the existing capacitor nor merely moving it closer closes F02. Resolve U3, then select the appropriate ceramic, its voltage/temperature/capacitance behavior and native footprint. Do not silently substitute it in the BOM.

**Tube-stage returns.** Merlin Blencowe's [Grounding chapter](https://www.valvewizard.co.uk/Grounding.pdf) treats amplifier grounding through physical signal and power-current paths: noisy rectifier/reservoir charging loops and low-level stages need deliberate separation, with stage returns related to their supply filtering. Our resulting rule is to review each loop across board and harness boundaries. This is compatible with four functional blocks. Protective earth is a separate safety connection and must not depend on removable wood or audio-return experiments.

**Mixed-signal advice has a scope.** [Analog Devices' mixed-signal PCB layout guidance](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/analog-dialogue/articles/what-are-the-basic-guidelines-for-layout-design-of-mixed-signal-pcbs.html) concerns mixed analog/digital designs and emphasizes floorplanning and return control. It does not establish that this low-frequency, high-voltage tube amplifier needs a particular layer count or arbitrary analog/digital ground split. Keep logic/coil current paths away from sensitive audio while checking their actual connection points.

**KiCad defaults are not minimum rules.** [KiCad 10 PCB Editor documentation, net classes](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) explains that netclass track/via settings supply routing defaults; they are not automatically minimum/maximum constraints. We therefore need explicit current-derived constraints and native verification. Assigning a thicker default alone is insufficient, especially at necks and transitions.

**Insulation and heat need inputs.** [TI's high-voltage insulation application note](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sdaa268/sdaa268.pdf) identifies working/transient voltage, insulation category and environmental/material factors as inputs to spacing decisions. Its isolated-device/power-converter examples are not the governing amp standard and its numerical examples are not copied here. [IPC-2152's public contents](https://www.electronics.org/TOC/IPC-2152.pdf) identify conductor, board and thermal variables; only the public contents were inspected, not the full sizing charts. Consequently this research does not certify any existing trace's ampacity or our 5.5 mm rule.

## Repair sequence and handoff

1. Close exact device/package decisions and circuit changes, including supervisor bypass, relay suppression and expression control. Keep the new [reverb part dossier](../../../component-selection/evidence/reverb-repair-picks-2026-11-21.md) as conditional evidence; it does not automatically release or substitute parts.
2. Allocate major-part, heat, wiring and tool envelopes in the delivered cabinet; re-place local circuits within the four blocks. Do not attach chassis loads to removable rear wood.
3. Derive current/insulation rules and the return/harness plan. Then complete routing, parity and native checks.
4. Verify populated fit and assembly/service paths, then run selected-part simulations and staged physical checks. Drill dimensions remain deferred by the user; useful electrical work continues.

Three bounded corner repairs and restored check severities have been adopted and are tracked in the [remediation checkpoint](../../../../brain/records/VAL-019-layout-reference-and-bounded-repairs.md). Trial capacitor moves were rejected: local courtyard interference remained, and the newly identified ceramic requirement was not addressed. The active design must not inherit those trial placements.
