VS-002 · 26 November 2026 · Conditional engineering check

Wider copper with more clearance

Move the ground route outward before widening it. The revised candidate doubles the two C607-adjacent ground sections from 0.3 to 0.6 mm and increases nominal pad clearance from 0.40 to 0.85 mm. The ordinary board rules pass; the stricter voltage screen still reports 47 findings on the tube-derived dry paths. Do not release this board.

Local repair

The vertical ground route moves from x = 143.0 to 143.6 mm. Its upper arc translates by 0.6 mm without changing radius. Its lower chamfer remains 45 degrees. Component positions, mounting holes and functional connections stay unchanged.

C607 pad 2 has centre x = 141.5 mm and nominal diameter 1.9 mm. The new nominal copper gap is:

\[g=(143.6-141.5)-\frac{1.9}{2}-\frac{0.6}{2}=0.85\,\mathrm{mm}\]

The old gap was \(1.5-0.95-0.15=0.40\,\mathrm{mm}\). This calculation uses nominal geometry; manufacturing tolerance and complete insulation qualification remain open.

Voltage basis

Check the current 14-part value/pin map against the existing protection model. Sweep four supply values, 64 resistor-tolerance corners and four clamp conditions: \(4\times64\times4=1024\) DC cases. Solve the same three nodes independently with Kirchhoff's current law; maximum disagreement is \(5.18\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{V}\).

Conditional rule test

Use 2.5 mm for FX_RETURN, IN_AC and MIX_OUT; retain IN_AC because the coupling capacitor can pass a transient. Use 0.64 mm for protected WET_AC and WET_Z_JOIN. Put the stronger dry rule last so it governs pairs that match both rules. These are screening rules, not an approved insulation design.

The numerical screening basis is the uncoated-board comparison in TI SLUP421, slide 24. Insulation role, transient voltage, material, environment and creepage must be assessed separately; see TI SLUP419. Do not credit solder mask as qualified protective insulation.
