HI-006 · 26 November 2026 · Conditional electrical screen

Do not classify every pot lead as low voltage

Several panel pots sit behind coupling capacitors connected to tube-stage DC. A shorted capacitor can put DC onto their harnesses. The candidate connector and wire ratings do not qualify the pot, its insulated joints or the assembled panel.

What was checked

1050 nominal linear DC networks cover ten listed capacitor shorts, intact-capacitor controls and electrical wiper fractions 0, 0.5 and 1. Fractions represent resistance division, not shaft angle or audio taper. Resistor values and capacitor endpoints come from the current circuit. Zero-resistance endpoints are joined exactly. The native boards and circuit are not edited.

The model opens intact capacitors at DC and omits leakage, nonlinear tube behavior and signal transients. It is a fault-path screen, not a complete single-fault or safety analysis. It does not establish actual maximum voltages, hot ratings, local track heating or the final buffered effects-path behavior.

  • clean: Cold/open first tube; actual R4 and R5 feed retained; grid current omitted. Inactive channel grounds CL_G2.
  • dirty_gain: Cold/open first tube; R12 feed retained. Later grid current omitted; inactive channel grounds DR_G2.
  • dirty_tone: Ideal fixed cathode-follower output voltage; actual R23 retained. Active mode uses normalled native effects path; C607 blocks wet-output DC.
  • pi_backfeed: Fixed PI-tail voltage drives R25 and failed C19 backward through the native normalled effects path. Future buffered path needs a new review.
  • presence: Fixed PI-tail voltage through R28; secondary has zero DC. This is not a direct B+ feed.
  • reverb: Fixed recovery-output voltage; unloaded wiper only. Real op-amp current limit, mute and summing input are excluded; no worst-case power claim.

How the calculation scales

Each source boundary is normalized to 1 V. For a particular setting and fault, the circuit gives terminal coefficient \(\alpha\) and total track coefficient \(\beta\):

\[|V_{\mathrm{terminal}}|=\alpha |V_s|,\qquad P_{\mathrm{track}}=\beta V_s^2.\]

A comparison power \(P_c\) gives \(V_{s,c}=\sqrt{P_c/\beta}\). The 0.25 W comparison comes from an earlier A1M customer-sheet review; it is not an approved rating for every proposed pot. Partial-track and wiper limits require separate evidence.

Example: C8 short, RV4 wiper at ground, first tube open. The actual 100 kΩ feed and 1 MΩ pot give:

\[V_{\mathrm{RV4,1}}=V_{B\_EARLY}\frac{1\,\mathrm{M\Omega}}{100\,\mathrm{k\Omega}+1\,\mathrm{M\Omega}}=0.909091V_{B\_EARLY}.\]

With an illustrative 300 V source, that is 272.727 V DC. This example is not a measured operating point or a qualified supply maximum.

Component decision

Alpha's RV24AF family specification lists 250 V AC maximum operation. Its 500 V AC, one-minute dielectric test is not a continuous operating rating. A DC working limit for the exact supplied variants remains unresolved. The customer sheet used in the earlier parts review does not close every suffix or taper limit.

Keep the existing pot values and functional blocks. Do not approve lower-voltage wire because normal DC is blocked, and do not claim a 600 V connector protects a lower-rated pot. Resolve the actual source bounds, exact pot DC/track/wiper limits and required fault containment before connector adoption. Compare any added blocking component against frequency response, failure modes and populated fit before changing the circuit.

Vocabulary key

Coupling capacitor
A capacitor that passes changing signals while blocking steady DC in normal operation.
DC
A voltage or current component that does not alternate polarity.
Track
The resistive element inside a potentiometer.
Wiper
The moving contact on the track.
Source impedance
The electrical resistance and reactance that limit current from a source.
Stiff source
An ideal source whose voltage does not fall when loaded.
Normalized
Expressed for a unit input so another input can be calculated by scaling.
Dielectric test
A specified insulation test; it does not establish a continuous working voltage.
PI
Phase inverter: the stage that drives the two output tubes with opposite signals.
Normalled path
The jack contact path used when no external plug interrupts it.
