# Panel interface audit evidence
Inputs: current circuit, boards, candidate library footprint and parent connector contract.
Process: generated by `../tools/check_panel_interfaces.py`; rerun after source changes, do not hand-edit.
Outputs: `pot-connectors.csv` and `checks.json`, with input hashes, endpoint gaps and hold status.
Human check: source agreement is not physical pinout, crimp, insulation, stock or service-access acceptance. These gates remain open.

`cable-loading.json`, `cable-loading-example.cir` and `cable-loading.log` come from `../tools/cable_loading.py`. JSON retains complete matched settings, deck/input hashes, controls, aggregate and refinement results. The example deck is illustrative, not the selected circuit; the factory regenerates every case. Retain one current output set. See `../cable-loading-review.md` for the model scope and placement implications.

`mating-geometry-checks.json` and `mating-geometry.svg` are generated by `../tools/check_mating_geometry.py`; zero actual placements and unknown service dimensions must remain unqualified.

`wire-dressing-checks.json` and `wire-dressing.svg` are generated by `../tools/check_wire_dressing.py`. Axial reservations use an explicit engineering assumption; they are not a populated collision or service test.
