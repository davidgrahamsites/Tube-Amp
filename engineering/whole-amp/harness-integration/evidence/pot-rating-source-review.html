Engineering consequence

The HI-006 generic-tube screen gives approximately 0.162 W at RV7 with C17 short and fixed B_PRE of 267.11 V; the 355 V reference gives approximately 0.290 W. Choosing the 0.5 W source would change the apparent comparison without resolving the actual supplied part. Neither result establishes the maximum continuous-setting, temperature or tolerance stress.

Full-track watts cannot replace a partial-track or wiper-current check. For a used segment, calculate \(P_s=I_s^2R_s\), then compare it with the manufacturer's allowed loading for that segment, temperature and duration. Do not assign a universal proportional derating rule to this carbon track without evidence.

Close these items before adopting the harness

  1. Match the supplied exact pot and drawing revision to resistance, taper, shaft and suffix.
  2. Obtain applicable DC working voltage, track-power temperature curve, partial-track and wiper-current limits.
  3. Compare normal signal and declared fault stress with those limits; include the source and protection behavior.
  4. If the pot cannot meet the limits, compare a same-function part or a local circuit correction, then repeat the affected electrical and panel-fit checks.
