# Engineering fabrication preview — not an order package

Native reverb PCB and its standalone schematic are complete as a checked layout draft. Gerber and drill exports under `fabrication-preview/` are for CAM review. Do not order until exact component envelopes, cabinet fit and circuit qualification are resolved in the parent build plan.

Board target: 160 x 100mm,2 layers,1.6mm FR-4,1oz finished copper,soldermask both sides,front silkscreen. No mains/HV copper. Four3.2mm NPTH mounting holes. PTH/NPTH drill files are separate and use the same absolute origin as Gerbers. Board dimensions and settings must be confirmed with the chosen manufacturer. Prefer a single documented stackup and compare Gerbers/drill maps in its CAM viewer before approving manufacture.

Do not confuse this daughterboard with a complete amplifier PCB. Main amplifier, secondary power supply and controller construction are separate assemblies. No supplier upload/order has been performed.
