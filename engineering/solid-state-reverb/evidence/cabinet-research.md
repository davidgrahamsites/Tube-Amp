## Fit implications

The 312mm opening is crowded for the source's nine operating tone/volume/presence knobs plus REVERB, input and switching. Keep the cabinet unchanged; use a custom chassis and consider two rows of smaller controls within the existing opening, subject to actual access and pot body clearance. Do not assume a stock 5E3 three-knob chassis fits this circuit.

PCB envelopes are provisional design envelopes. A 120 x 90mm reverb module fits within the *external* cabinet envelope; that alone proves nothing about free space around speaker, transformers, tubes, harness bends or ventilation. Require the chassis/speaker envelope and full-size placement proof before drilling or purchasing a chassis.
