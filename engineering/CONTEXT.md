# Engineering artifacts

One job: retain editable designs and their evidence for the Renegade project and its supporting equipment.

## Inputs
[Workspace contract](../CONTEXT.md), [handoff](../Renegade_Codex_Handoff.md), [status](../PROJECT_STATUS.md), and relevant [brain records](../brain/INDEX.md).

## Process
Each engineering module has one contract, explicit revision/release status and linked inputs. Use the electrical/physical skill ladders. Keep proposed calculations separate from hardware evidence; preserve imported sources.

## Outputs
[Whole amplifier](whole-amp/CONTEXT.md), [current received-tank circuit](whole-amp/reverb-600/CONTEXT.md), [component selection](component-selection/CONTEXT.md) and [mechanical fit](whole-amp/mechanical/CONTEXT.md) own the active prototype work. [Materials catalog](../brain/catalog/WORKSPACE.md) distinguishes all revisions and evidence.

[Updated schematic](updated-schematic/CONTEXT.md): editable source baseline and 220 V /50 Hz tube-reverb draft, with native validation and scoped simulation evidence.

[Tube matcher](tube-matcher/CONTEXT.md): standalone EL34/12AX7 characterization and pairing instrument design.

[Reusable functional-block datasheets](functional-blocks/CONTEXT.md): final circuit-module operating and interface contracts, companion compatibility and revisioned reuse evidence required by DEC-018.

## Human check
Review the affected module's schematic, component stresses, physical implementation and actual validation before fabrication or energization.
