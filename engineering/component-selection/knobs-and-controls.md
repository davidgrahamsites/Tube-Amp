# Pointer knobs and matching controls

Research date: 2026-11-11. User preference: the supplied black sculpted pointer knob with white index. Preserve this visual direction; it is not an instruction to buy an unidentified image match.

## Preferred sourcing candidate

**Mable MAKP0437S**, advertised as Sato Parts K-2195 small, nominal 26 mm diameter × 16.5 mm height. [Supplier listing](https://www.mableaudio.com/en/productview.asp?sid=10588). Listed USD 1.65 each at quantity ten: USD 16.50 for the ten faceplate controls, excluding delivery and any domestic quote adjustment. Inventory and Shanghai delivery are not confirmed by this catalog price.

The supplier photo shows a black body, white pointer and metal bore insert. Its [dimension drawing](https://www.mableaudio.com/uploadFile/Picture_small/202206231513104017.jpg) shows 25.95 mm base diameter and 16.50 mm height, and labels shaft-hole options **6 mm and 6.4 mm**. Therefore the order must specify a bore variant; do not assume a single interchangeable size. Screw and insert materials remain unverified. Local photos and drawing are in `evidence/knob-MAKP0437S*.jpg`.

The [actual Sato K-2195 manufacturer specification](https://www.satoparts.co.jp/ENG/products/search/K/K-2195) identifies a phenolic body and, for S size, two M3.5 retaining screws, recommended torque 0.5 N·m and 1.5 mm hex tool. The [manufacturer catalog](https://www.satoparts.co.jp/dcms_media/other/LINE-UP-CATALOG_2024.06.pdf) specifies a 6 mm shaft and 26 mm S-size diameter. These specifications apply to genuine Sato parts. Mable's additional 6.4 mm variant is not automatically covered by Sato documentation. Ask Mable whether this is genuine Sato or a compatible reproduction and obtain the supplied variant drawing.

## Potentiometer interface

Prefer a robust panel-mounted solder-lug pot with smooth solid shaft and a screw-secured knob. A 6 mm bore does **not** fit a 6.35 mm shaft. A 6.4 mm variant is a potential match for a 6.35 mm shaft, subject to actual bore tolerance and screw engagement. Do not force a split/splined shaft into this choice or drill knobs to compensate for an ordering error.

Taiwan Alpha RV24AF is a candidate family. [Manufacturer data](https://www.taiwanalpha.com/downloads?id=104&target=products) specifies 300±5° rotation and 15,000 cycles. Supplier descriptions disagree about operating voltage versus dielectric test voltage; use manufacturer ratings, not a reseller's 500 V withstand figure as an operating rating. Exact domestic-stock part suffixes, tapers, shaft length and panel bushing remain to be selected.

The internal bias and send trims are not included in the ten faceplate knobs.

## Fit consequence

For a preliminary 32 mm shaft pitch, 26 mm knobs have about 6 mm edge clearance. Ten in one row span 314 mm including the end knob radii, before margins, switches and sockets. That already exceeds the cabinet's published 312 mm straight opening. This is a planning check, not a claim that the opening equals the final panel width. Do not freeze a one-row faceplate. The 22 mm MAKP0437SS is a smaller visual-family alternative, but its exact supplied geometry, bore and stock still need checking. Cabinet usable dimensions remain a supplier dependency.

## Release conditions

Confirm exact knob variant and manufacturer, screw retention, ten-piece availability and domestic quote; select matching pots and verify their shaft/bushing drawings; complete panel sweep/interference checks. No knob or pot is released for ordering or drilling yet.
