HD-007 · 26 November 2026 · Candidate only

Series pass protection and absolute output limit

The sampled pass-short defect is corrected in the DC model; full protection is not complete. Two independently referenced series pass devices hold regulator differential below 18.784 V when either device has a drain-to-source short. A separate short inside the regulator raises the modeled output to 437.93 V. An independent absolute output-voltage limit is still required. No native schematic, PCB or part selection changed.

What changed

Add a second depletion MOSFET in series, with its own floating ATL431 reference. Each reference uses a 1.24 MΩ/200 kΩ divider for about 18 V above output, a 149.7 kΩ feed and a 100 kΩ gate resistor. Opposed nominal 12 V Zeners limit each gate-to-source voltage. Retain the 100 Ω drain feed and 680 Ω source resistor. Add a 100 kΩ output preload.

These are component functions and nominal simulation values, not qualified packages. Each high-voltage resistor may require series parts for voltage, heat and spacing. The gate-clamp current also enters the output through its reference; include it in the load balance.

Why the preload is needed

With no preload, the balanced 550 V cold-load/Q1-short probe gives only 3.847 mA through the regulator output, below its allocated 5 mA minimum. With the preload, all 270 declared cases exceed 5.583 mA. Maximum modeled preload heat is 0.854 W; select and derate the actual resistor before placing it.

\[I_S=\frac{V_S-V_{REGIN}}{R_S}\] \[I_{BIAS}=\sum_{j=1}^{2}\frac{V_{IN}-V_{REFj}}{149.7\,\mathrm{k\Omega}},\qquad I_{BACK}=\sum_{j=1}^{2}\frac{V_{Gj}-V_{REFj}}{100\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}\] \[I_S+I_{BIAS}+I_{BACK}=\frac{V_O}{78\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}+I_{WARM}+\frac{V_{ADJ}}{R_L}+\frac{V_O}{100\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}\]

Subtract adjust-pin current from source current when checking regulator output current. Set the extra warm-load current to zero in the cold fixture. The fixed warm resistor represents one nominal load only; it is not a complete tube model.

Conditional bound and actual parts

A useful design requirement is to keep each reference no more than 19 V above output and require the healthy transistor to be off by 5 V negative gate bias at the specified temperature. If the healthy stage must carry more than its off-state leakage and the source resistor has a nonnegative drop, the regulator differential is bounded by:

\[V_{REGIN}-V_O\le19+5=24\,\mathrm V<40\,\mathrm V\]

This is conditional reasoning. Full-temperature reference accuracy at the available low bias, exact MOSFET limits, reverse operation and transient overshoot remain to be checked. It does not prove the bounds merely by stating them.

Vishay BZX85C12, revision 2.9, specifies up to 12.7 V at its 20 mA test current and a positive temperature coefficient. Its forward curve is typical, so the opposed-pair maximum cannot be certified from the nominal 12 V model. No clamp part is selected.

The Microchip LR8 does not directly cover the 550 V diagnostic input and full load envelope. The SCHURTER SHF series is rated up to 500 VDC with a 0.5 A minimum catalog current; it does not establish branch protection here. Neither alternative is adopted.

Next correction

Define an absolute output-voltage detector that remains effective if the regulator shorts, then verify its control path, current and timing limits. Check input collapse and reverse-current paths separately. Keep the functional power block intact. Qualify exact parts, heat, socket/carrier geometry and sourcing before applying the circuit to native CAD or the populated enclosure.

Vocabulary key

Pass device
A transistor controlling current through a supply path.
Preload
A deliberate load that keeps a regulator above its minimum operating current.
Operating point
The circuit's steady voltages and currents.
Seed
An initial numerical guess used by the solver.
Current balance
Currents entering and leaving a node must add to zero.
Absolute output limit
A ceiling measured against circuit ground, independent of the main regulator's setting.
Differential voltage
The voltage between two specified terminals.
Depletion MOSFET
A transistor that conducts at zero gate-to-source voltage.
