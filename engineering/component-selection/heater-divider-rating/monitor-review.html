Monitor supply and fault hold

The corrected monitor and one initialized fault test work in the declared model. Startup and the 320 V coupled case remain unresolved. Do not adopt this circuit or release a PCB from this evidence.

Changes

Feed the monitor from the unregulated input through three 22 kΩ resistors. Restore the 100 kΩ output preload. The monitor must retain power while the protected output falls; the regulator still needs its minimum load.

Use the spare LM2903B comparator to pull the main comparator threshold low after a fault. A 10 kΩ resistor separates the 2.5 V reference from this pull-down. The hold comparator compares one-third of the main comparator output with half the reference voltage. This gives deep hysteresis; it is not a power-cycle latch.

The first 40 kΩ/20 kΩ feedback divider drew current through the optocoupler LEDs without a fault. Changing it to 2 MΩ/1 MΩ reduced nominal idle LED current from 64.975 µA to the model floor. Bias and board leakage must still be checked at temperature.

Model and source limits

The TI LM2903B model is version 1.0, dated 26 January 2023. Its original bytes and copyright are preserved. The generated deck removes TC=0,0 and uses ngspice ngbehavior=psa for whole-deck PSpice translation. TI deliberately drives the model output to midsupply outside allowed input or supply ranges; the real part does not behave that way.

LM4040CIM3-8.2/NOPB and REF3425 use finite-current voltage/current surrogates. Optocoupler LEDs, transfer ratio, NPN transistors and pass devices remain simplified. Typical delay is not a maximum fault-response guarantee. SMD ICs still need qualified removable carriers.

Then check reverse current, hot actuator limits, exact parts and carrier heat before native adoption and populated chassis fit. Functional block boundaries remain unchanged.

Vocabulary key

Shunt regulator
A device that holds voltage by taking surplus current.
Preload
A resistor that provides minimum load current.
Comparator
A circuit that switches its output according to which input voltage is higher.
Hysteresis
Different thresholds for entering and leaving a state.
Fault hold
A control state that keeps protection active after detection.
Surrogate
A simplified model used where a qualified device model is unavailable.
Current balance
The check that all currents entering and leaving a node sum to zero.
Carrier
A removable adapter board that holds a surface-mount IC.
