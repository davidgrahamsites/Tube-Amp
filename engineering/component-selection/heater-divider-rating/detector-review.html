Precision detector and preamp setpoint

HD-009 · 26 November 2026 · Baseline 222ccd7 · Candidate only; no native adoption or protection release.

Carry the 267.11 V nominal preamp candidate into the next protection check. It creates room for detector error without raising the 300 V ceiling. The tested small-signal gain changes by less than 0.1 dB. Preserve the power-stage supply and the power, audio and reverb block boundaries.

Changes under test

  • Reduce the regulator lower programming resistance from 43.2 kΩ to 42.2 kΩ; keep the same reference/current/tolerance profiles.
  • Use REF3425IDBVR and LM2903BIDR as detector candidates. Both require removable socket carriers; the TI B comparator is not a PDIP part.
  • Sense output through four 294 kΩ upper resistors and one 10.1 kΩ lower resistor.
  • Replace the 100 kΩ output preload in this trial with a monitor feed of three 22.1 kΩ resistors and an ideal 8.2 V shunt rail.
  • Drive two optocoupler/NPN paths that pull the gate-reference nodes toward the output. CTR 25% and transistor gain 20 are model allocations, not hot-part guarantees.

The reference error allocation combines initial tolerance, box-method temperature drift, line/load regulation and an additional 200 ppm allowance for solder, hysteresis and aging. That last allowance requires qualification. Divider resistance uses an allocated ±0.4% total error; exact resistor parts and their service profile remain open.

\[e_R=0.0005+6\times10^{-6}(165)+15\times10^{-6}(9.5)+30\times10^{-6}(0.03)+0.0002=0.0018334\]

The 165 °C span follows the reference's box-method specification; using only distance from room temperature would understate this bound. A planned 100 kΩ reference-output preload draws 25 µA and keeps the reference sourcing current despite comparator bias. Its load is included in the 0.95 mA monitor quiet-current allocation.

\[V_T=(V_R+V_{OS})\left(1+\frac{R_H}{R_L}\right)+I_{\mathrm{error}}R_H\]

Use \(R_H=4(294\,\mathrm{k\Omega})\), \(R_L=10.1\,\mathrm{k\Omega}\), comparator offset ±4 mV, input-current allowance ±62.5 nA and board-leakage allowance ±100 nA. These allocations give the table's trip interval. The old 292.156 V normal upper allocation overlaps it; the revised setpoint does not.

Coupled DC checks

The 512 cases combine 320/550 V input, low/high programming profiles, cold/warm load, no fault or either pass-device short or regulator short, both trip endpoints, independent −2.5/−5 V pass thresholds, and 646/714 Ω source resistance. The threshold and transistor ranges remain sensitivities, not a full statistical or temperature model.

All retained cases satisfy explicit current balance. Minimum regulator output current is 6.679 mA where the regulator is intact. The ideal monitor rail absorbs current in every retained case; it does not supply hidden power to the high-voltage output.

At maximum allocated LED drive, the separate rail budget retains:

\[I_{\mathrm{reserve}}=\frac{251.8806-9}{3(22100)(1.05)}-0.95\,\mathrm{mA}-1.5\,\mathrm{mA}=1.039\,\mathrm{mA}\]

This only checks steady current availability for an allocated 7.5–9 V rail and ±5% feed resistance. It does not select a shunt regulator, prove startup, or qualify resistor heat and voltage ratings.

Numerical correction

The direct floating NPN representation returned an invalid 0.0134 V operating point with 7.557 mA current-balance error. Reject that result. An equivalent local-voltage wrapper evaluates the same NPN model near ground and returns its terminal currents to the floating circuit. Six fixtures at 0/300 V common-mode agree within 1 nA; the largest difference is about 300 pA. The wrapper changes numerical coordinates, not device physics or ratings.

Parts and release boundary

TI REF34, SBAS804G supplies the reference accuracy, temperature, regulation and current limits. TI LM393 family, SLCS005AH supplies the LM2903B limits and package evidence. Its propagation delay is typical; it is not a guaranteed fault-response bound. Comparator common-mode behavior and the allocated reference aging allowance still need validation.

China listing identities are REF3425IDBVR, C187836 and LM2903BIDR, C2869804. Stock, low-quantity price, domestic delivery and carriers are unconfirmed; no purchase is requested.

4,427 evidence checks pass. The controller uses continuous LED-current interpolation; it does not implement a real comparator transition, hysteresis or fault latch. Vocabulary key

Setpoint
The voltage the regulator tries to maintain.
Trip threshold
The voltage at which protection is commanded.
CTR
Optocoupler collector current divided by LED current.
Box-method drift
Maximum reference-voltage spread divided by the full specified temperature span.
Common mode
A circuit's voltage relative to ground, separate from the voltage between its terminals.
Shunt rail
A low-voltage supply controlled by diverting excess current to ground.
Fault latch
A circuit that retains a fault command until a defined reset.
Small signal
A linear response near the operating point; it excludes clipping.
Socket carrier
A small adapter board that makes a surface-mount part replaceable through a socket.

Before adoption, recheck large-signal clipping, driver headroom and the approximately 25 W target at the lower preamp setpoint; the AC comparison does not establish these.
