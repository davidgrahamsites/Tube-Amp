Preamp setpoint and large-signal output

HD-010 · 26 November 2026 · Baseline 457b5f2 · Candidate comparison; no native adoption.

The proposed 267.11 V setpoint retains the previous approximately 25 W output scale in this model. At the same near-target input, fundamental power decreases by 1.22% clean and 1.48% dirty. Distortion remains about 5.6–5.7%; this is not a clean 25 W rating.

Comparison

Each pair uses the same input, model and controls: 1 kHz sine, volume 1, tone and gain 0.5, 8 Ω resistive load, stiff 400 V main and 380 V screen supplies. The preamp source changes from 273.41 V to 267.11 V. Input amplitudes differ between channels because their gains differ. These inputs do not define the full guitar or pedal envelope.

How the result is checked

Sixteen isolated simulations reach 1 s. Save the last 20 cycles and compare consecutive ten-cycle windows. Repeat the two proposed-voltage near-target cases with half the maximum timestep. The independent checker reads the saved waveforms, calculates sine/cosine projections for ten harmonics and reconstructs power and distortion; it does not reuse the simulator metric function.

For an integer number of sampled cycles:

\[A_h=\frac{2}{N}\left|\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}v_n e^{-j2\pi h f t_n}\right|,\qquad P_1=\frac{A_1^2}{2R_L}\]

\[\mathrm{THD}_{2:10}=100\frac{\sqrt{\sum_{h=2}^{10}A_h^2}}{A_1}\%\]

For the proposed-voltage clean near-target case:

\[P_1=\frac{(19.67638\,\mathrm V)^2}{2(8\,\Omega)}\approx24.1975\,\mathrm W\]

All 205 evidence checks pass, including source/native hashes, exact case coverage, source placement, load, completed time, finite values, saved-waveform identity, harmonic calculations, settling and timestep refinement. Refined power changes by less than 0.033%. The checks establish the reported comparison, not hardware performance.

Decision and remaining work

The user’s approximately 25 W target is unchanged. Its final distortion criterion and measured operating conditions remain unresolved; this comparison does not lower the target or clear that requirement.

Keep 267.11 V as the next protection candidate. Do not revise native schematics or routing until the real monitor supply, fault latch or hysteresis, startup, response overshoot, reverse-current paths and hot actuator limits are resolved. Preserve the power, audio and reverb functional blocks.

The supply is ideal in these audio runs. The transformer and tube models are not exact selected hardware, and the test does not cover other controls, broadband stability, mains sag, noise, burst recovery or temperature. Final power must be measured with stated distortion, bandwidth and load conditions.

Next monitor-supply research lead: the TI LM4040-N family includes an 8.192 V shunt option. Exact grade, package, China source, socket carrier, startup and full current/thermal limits remain to be selected and checked; this is not an adopted part.

Vocabulary key

Fundamental
The output component at the input test frequency.
Harmonic
An output component at a whole-number multiple of the test frequency.
THD
Total harmonic distortion; here, harmonics 2 through 10 relative to the fundamental.
Headroom
The available signal swing before unacceptable clipping or distortion.
Stiff supply
A simulated supply whose voltage does not fall as load current increases.
Timestep refinement
Repeating a simulation with a smaller time step to check numerical sensitivity.
Setpoint
The target regulator output voltage.
Shunt
A device that regulates voltage by drawing current through a parallel path.
