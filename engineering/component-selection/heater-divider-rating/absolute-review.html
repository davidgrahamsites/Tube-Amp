Absolute protection: control authority

HD-008 · 26 November 2026 · Input revision d710958 · Forced-on simulation only; not an automatic protection circuit.

Use a direct gate-reference sink for the next detector design. Reject the upper-divider shunt as the sole protection actuator: two sampled cases exceed the 300 V ceiling. Keep the power-supply functional block intact; native schematics and boards are unchanged.

Question and test boundary

HD-007 retained a regulator-short overvoltage. This study forces protection on and asks whether its control path can reduce the resulting steady output. It does not model the detector, its supply, switching delay or peak voltage.

The 192 cases combine three control paths, 320/550 V inputs, low/high regulator programming profiles, cold/warm loads, both pass thresholds at −2.5/−5 V, and 646/714 Ω source resistance. The −5 V value is an allocated cutoff sensitivity, not a guaranteed hot-device bound. The MOSFET and gate-clamp models remain simplified.

Each direct sink returns current to the output node. It has a 0.9 V offset, 0.01 S slope and 5 mA limit. Its maximum sampled current is 2.733 mA. The single-sink test retains both healthy pass devices and the shorted regulator; it is not a simultaneous pass-short test.

\[I_{\mathrm{sink}}=\min\left(5\,\mathrm{mA},\max\left(0,\frac{V_{\mathrm{GREF}}-V_{\mathrm{OUT}}-0.9\,\mathrm V}{100\,\Omega}\right)\right)\]

This equation defines the required actuator interface. An optocoupler alone has not been selected to meet it; a gain stage or a different drive current needs checking.

Detector tolerance conflict

The existing high programming profile gives the following normal-output upper allocation. Voltages and resistances come from the unchanged HD-006 contract.

\[V_{\mathrm{normal,max}}=1.313\left(1+\frac{43416}{199}\right)+(101\,\mu\mathrm A)(43416\,\Omega)=292.156\,\mathrm V\]

For a TL431BI screen, combine the 25 °C initial limits with the full-temperature deviation conservatively. Relative to 2.495 V, the resulting reference factors are:

\[k_L=\frac{2.483-0.034}{2.495}=0.981563,\qquad k_H=\frac{2.507+0.034}{2.495}=1.018437\]

Let \(T\) be nominal trip voltage. Avoiding a trip at the normal upper allocation and keeping the trip below the ceiling require:

\[T>\frac{292.156}{k_L}=297.644\,\mathrm V,\qquad T<\frac{300}{k_H}=294.569\,\mathrm V\]

No value meets both inequalities. These necessary conditions already fail before divider error, input current, cathode-voltage effects, detector delay or overshoot. Compare a tighter detector with a lower regulated setpoint; do not raise the ceiling to make this screen pass.

Current-balance check

The checker reconstructs source current and both bias returns, including gate-clamp backflow. It compares them with the heater divider, regulator programming path, preload and warm load.

\[I_S+\sum I_{\mathrm{bias}}+\sum I_{\mathrm{back}}=\frac{V_O}{78\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}+\frac{V_{\mathrm{ADJ}}}{R_{\mathrm{low}}}+\frac{V_O}{100\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}+I_{\mathrm{warm}}\]

Maximum balance error is 0.082 nA. All 933 evidence checks pass, including retained failures and native-file hashes. A passing evidence check does not mean the proposed circuit passes protection requirements. Solver logs retain fallback warnings.

Manufacturer evidence

Vishay VO618A, document 83432, revision 2.1: DIP-4 option; minimum CTR is 50% at 1 mA LED current and 5 V collector voltage. Maximum saturation voltage is 0.4 V at 1 mA LED current and 0.25 mA collector current, at 25 °C. These test points do not qualify a 2.733 mA hot sink.

TI TL431, SLVS543S: B-grade reference limits are 2483–2507 mV at 25 °C and 10 mA; the BI temperature-deviation limit is 34 mV. Typical drift is not a worst-case guarantee.

Next action and evidence

Close the detector tolerance, LED supply and actuator component bounds; then check startup, fault overshoot, reverse current and input collapse. Exact China parts, sockets, thermal design and populated chassis fit remain open.

Vocabulary key

Actuator
The circuit that changes current after a fault is detected.
Gate reference
The voltage that controls a pass transistor’s gate.
CTR
Optocoupler collector current divided by LED current.
Cutoff
A transistor condition that greatly reduces conduction; leakage remains.
Forced-on
The simulation applies the control action without a detector.
Steady state
The settled condition; it excludes startup and transition peaks.
Current balance
Current entering a node equals current leaving it.
Preload
A load retained when the amplifier draws little current.
