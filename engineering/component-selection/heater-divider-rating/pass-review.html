HD-006 · 26 November 2026 · Study only

Preamp pass-stage model and protection hold

Do not adopt this circuit yet. Normal DC calculations support the candidate topology. A pass-transistor short exceeds regulator and gate ratings. Independent fault protection, reverse-current control and physical qualification remain open. Native schematic and PCB files are unchanged.

Candidate circuit

The experiment places a 100 Ω resistor before a depletion MOSFET and a 680 Ω resistor after it. A floating ATL431 reference holds the gate about 20 V above the regulated output. A behavioral LM317L-N controls the output. This differs from HD-005's 680 Ω input-feed arrangement; its old heat and fault results cannot qualify the new arrangement.

Two 49.9 kΩ resistors feed the gate reference. A 700 kΩ/100 kΩ divider sets its voltage. A local 100 nF capacitor with 1 Ω series resistance bridges regulator input to output. The output capacitor is 100 µF. The explicit 78 kΩ heater divider and regulator programming network remain loads. A 36.2 kΩ resistor represents the extra nominal warm preamp load; it does not reproduce tube behavior across voltage, signal or temperature.

Model correction

The original TI ATL431 model remains byte-for-byte unchanged in sources. The generated deck translates PSpice IF, removes one unsupported zero capacitor temperature coefficient and implements an order-independent LIMIT expression. The floating adapter presents terminal voltages relative to the anode and returns the corresponding terminal currents. These are simulator adapters, not added circuit parts.

Candidate results

Run 54 DC cases: three diagnostic inputs (320, 380 and 550 V), three regulator setting profiles, warm/cold loads and three MOSFET threshold sensitivities. Normal simulated output follows the declared setting within 10 mV. Regulator differential spans 9.71–19.91 V; maximum modeled pass heat is 3.775 W. These diagnostic inputs are not verified transformer outputs.

Calculations and limits

The regulator setting and reference setting are:

\[V_O=V_{REF}\left(1+\frac{R_L}{R_S}\right)+I_{ADJ}R_L\] \[V_{KA}\approx2.5\left(1+\frac{700\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}{100\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}\right)=20\,\mathrm V\]

The source and gate-feed paths both supply the output. Check both when calculating load:

\[I_S=\frac{V_S-V_{REGIN}}{680\,\Omega},\quad I_B=\frac{V_{IN}-V_{GREF}}{99.8\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}\] \[I_S+I_B=\frac{V_O}{78\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}+I_{WARM}+\frac{V_{ADJ}}{R_L}\]

Use zero for the extra warm current in the cold fixture. During a transient, include capacitor currents. Resistor and pass-device heat use:

\[P_R=I^2R,\qquad P_Q=(V_D-V_S)I_D\]

The ATL431 data sheet and model serve different purposes: published limits constrain the design; the model's own notes exclude temperature and loop behavior. The MOSFET LEVEL1 model is only a generic fit. Sweeping threshold with fixed transconductance does not bound all real devices. The LM317L-N behavioral model omits reverse conduction, thermal protection and real dynamics; its 40 V differential limit must still be enforced.

The transistor's control, source and drain terminals.
Differential voltage

A voltage reference whose local return is above or below circuit ground.
Surrogate
A simplified substitute used for a limited question.
KCL
Kirchhoff's current law: currents entering and leaving a node balance.
