Why the guide was added

The project needs equipment requirements tied to actual test articles. The new HTML guide stages equipment by test and records capability, evidence, and safety limits. It is not a purchasing list of approved models or an energizing procedure.

The canonical reader page and self-contained export carry the same guide. Both include a vocabulary key and KaTeX measurement equations. No equipment model or seller is approved, and no physical test result is claimed.

Records and current status

Each test record must identify the test article, date, operator, exact parts, instrument and probe setup, calibration check, connection diagram, operating conditions, limits fixed before testing, raw readings, uncertainty, result, and any retest. This keeps a simulation, equipment capability, and physical measurement distinct.

The low-voltage monitor fixture is the first hardware test gate. It can check startup, reference, comparator outputs, trip, release, and interrupted power without the complete amplifier. It does not qualify full-supply overshoot, insulation, resistor heating, or fault interruption.

Vocabulary

Test article
The specific circuit, fixture, part, tank, or assembly being tested.
Current limit
A supply setting that restricts current during a test.
Oscilloscope
An instrument that records voltage against time.
Impedance
Opposition to alternating current, including its phase change.
Dummy load
A resistor assembly that absorbs amplifier output power in place of a speaker.
DRC
Design-rule check of a PCB layout.
Native adoption
Acceptance into the project's editable schematic or board files.
