Reverb pot connector candidate

November 26, 2026 · HI-008 · Candidate checks pass; adoption remains open

Purpose and result

HI-008 places a removable connector for the Reverb pot on a separate candidate board. The candidate preserves the three signal assignments: cavity 1 WET_TOP, cavity 2 WET_W, cavity 3 GND, and cavity 4 unassigned. The active board, schematic, and circuit are unchanged.

The corrected candidate parses in KiCad. Its recorded DRC run reports zero violations and zero open connections. This confirms only the declared candidate and rule set. It does not approve the connector, electrical loading, insulation, fabrication, or physical fit.

Placement and service space

JR4 pad 1 is at \((73,90.5)\;\mathrm{mm}\), rotated 180 degrees on the 160 mm × 100 mm board. The locator peg is 5.18 mm inside the lower edge. The wire-exit face is 7.07 mm beyond the edge.

Using the HI-004 local wire-exit coordinate and the HI-005 straight reserve:

\[y_{exit}=90.5-(-16.57)=107.07\;\mathrm{mm}\]
\[y_{restraint}=107.07+12.70=119.77\;\mathrm{mm}\]

The first-restraint line lies 19.77 mm beyond the board edge. This is a nominal straight-wire reservation. It is not a complete keepout. Bend radius, wire spread, latch access, withdrawal, cabinet clearance, and tool access remain open.

Route repair and checker correction

The first routing trial had 19 DRC findings and was rejected. The candidate still removes eight segments, adds twelve, and adds two vias. FB-169 widened every WET_TOP and WET_W candidate segment to 0.6 mm, including retained source segments. It also widened the new JR4 ground branch to 0.6 mm. Earlier 0.3 mm widths no longer describe these routes. The candidate uses 0.8 mm vias with 0.4 mm drills. The earlier 0.3 mm wet routes were not shown defective; they also pass this limited DC-drop screen. The 0.6 mm width adds conductor margin and lowers the calculated loss.

The existing project rules were preserved. The candidate adds a 0.6 mm minimum-width rule for the wet nets; no rule was weakened. Unrelated candidate-board items remain unchanged. The active Reverb board, circuit, and schematic remain unchanged.

The initial checker read KiCad's circle-drill enumeration as zero. The native enumeration is one. The checker now uses KiCad's named circle constant. The footprint hole geometry did not change to satisfy the corrected check. This was a checker defect, not a hardware change.

The revised candidate still adds trace length to WET_TOP and GND and reduces it on WET_W. These changes need a parasitic-loading comparison. The four-cavity symbol and unassigned-cavity metadata also need integration before native adoption.

FB-169 copper screen

The candidate copper calculation uses a two-layer, 1.6 mm board with 35 µm nominal outer copper. Its acceptance requirements are at least 30 µm finished foil and 20 µm via-barrel copper. A fabricator has not confirmed either minimum. The four active boards still have no explicit stackup. Only the separate Reverb candidate has an explicit nominal copper stackup; it has not been adopted.

The conditional calculation assumes 70 °C copper, 20% track-width reduction, and 0.25 mA screen current. The resistor envelope is \(18\;\mathrm V/80\;\mathrm{k\Omega}+18\;\mathrm V/980\;\mathrm{k\Omega}=0.243367\;\mathrm{mA}\). The difference is \(0.25-0.243367=0.006633\;\mathrm{mA}=6.633\;\mathrm{\mu A}\) in this calculation screen. This is a diagnostic source condition, not a fault or startup bound.

\[V_{drop}=IR,\quad V_{WET\_TOP}=0.25\;\mathrm{mA}(0.064953\;\Omega)=16.2383\;\mathrm{\mu V}\]
\[V_{WET\_W}=0.25\;\mathrm{mA}(0.064085\;\Omega)=16.0212\;\mathrm{\mu V}\]

Both values are below the screen’s 0.1 mV track/via-drop threshold. These values cover modeled track and via resistance only. Pad, contact, and harness resistance are excluded. The total-length method overbounds branched parallel paths. Temperature rise, fault current, startup current, and current capacity are not qualified. The inventory covers all four active boards, but this result screens only the Reverb candidate's wet nets; it does not qualify all boards or shared returns.

CU-002 supply-rail copper

The same separate candidate now uses 0.8 mm on all 79 straight segments and four arcs on +15V and −15V. The existing 0.6 mm WET_TOP/WET_W routes remain unchanged. The supply-path geometry, footprints, and mounting locations did not change. The active source board, circuit, and schematic remain unchanged.

The current check re-read passive values and complete op-amp pin maps, then recomputed 12 load cases with the retained pure model. It records the old whole-source hash as historical. At the 18 V diagnostic condition, the maximum rail allocation is 99.925 mA. This fits the 100 mA calculation screen under 25 °C quiescent-current conditions only. Hot, startup, and fault current remain unqualified.

The width screen uses 30 µm finished copper, 20% narrowing, 70 °C assumed copper, a 50 mV track-plus-via drop allowance per rail, and 1.5× margin on finished width. At 0.8 mm drawn width, the modeled drops are:

\[V_{+15}=0.100\;\mathrm A(0.267703\;\Omega)=26.7703\;\mathrm{mV},\quad V_{-15}=0.100\;\mathrm A(0.323276\;\Omega)=32.3276\;\mathrm{mV}\]
\[w_{drawn,required}=0.636744\;\mathrm{mm}\;( +15\;\mathrm V),\quad0.771494\;\mathrm{mm}\;( -15\;\mathrm V)\]

The −15 V rail sets the 0.8 mm choice. At 0.3 mm, both rails exceed the 50 mV allocation. At 0.6 mm, both drops fall below 50 mV, but neither rail reaches the 1.5× finished-width margin. The earlier 0.3 mm wet-signal result is separate: those tracks passed their lower-current diagnostic. These supply rails do not pass their stated screen at 0.3 mm.

A 1.0 mm straight-segment trial caused ten clearance findings and was rejected. A temporary export also produced 41 missing-library warnings; the library table was restored before the accepted 0.8 mm run. The first 0.8 mm factory omitted four segments and 15 arcs, so native preservation and current checks failed. The factory and checker were corrected to include PCB_ARC. No DRC finding was waived. The final candidate reports zero violations and zero opens. The supply review records 15 passing evidence checks and four passing negative controls, including rejection of the old 0.3 mm drop, the 0.6 mm margin, a narrow arc, and half-thickness copper.

This is a conditional DC-drop and geometry screen. It does not qualify thermal rise, hot copper, fault or startup current, pads, connectors, harness losses, other rails, or the other active-board nets. The all-board inventory is not an all-board qualification.

FB-170 / CU-003 broader copper pass

The user asked for wider copper where practical. The candidate uses 0.8 mm on +12V, COIL_REV, DRIVER, DRIVE_CAP, and TANK_IN. It uses 0.6 mm on SEND_BUF, REC_OUT, WET_BUF, SUM_INV, OUT_BUF, and OUT_AC. Most GND sections use 1.0 mm. At this CU-003 checkpoint, eight local GND necks use 0.6 or 0.8 mm; two original 0.3 mm sections beside C607 at the tube-side WET_AC node remain pending voltage-spacing review. VS-002 below supersedes that last width and geometry. SUM_INV is U12A's output; its sensitive SUM input was not widened.

Ten straight segments moved around K3 to retain clearance after widening: the +12V path moves 0.3 mm from the relay-coil pad, and the nearby ground path uses a 0.6 mm neck at y = 26.5 mm. One additional GND diagonal was added. No footprints, mount holes, or functional blocks moved. This is a separate candidate; the active board, schematic, and circuit remain unchanged.

Uniform widening first produced 17 findings: 11 clearance errors, three shorts, and three mask bridges. The corrected candidate reports zero DRC violations and zero opens. The refreshed HI-008 checker passes 37 checks and four controls. CU-003 passes 18 checks and three controls. No rule exception was added. These checks do not approve high-voltage spacing.

Vocabulary

Native adoption
Integration into the active schematic and board files.
DRC
Design-rule check of the PCB geometry and connections.
Open connection
A required electrical connection that is not routed to completion.
Via
A plated hole that connects copper layers.
Parasitic loading
Unintended capacitance or other electrical loading added by wiring and layout.
Restraint
A tie, clamp, or support that fixes the wire position.
Unassigned cavity
A connector contact position with no approved electrical connection.
Keepout
A defined region that other parts or wires must not enter.
Track resistance sum
The sum of estimated resistance for every copper segment on a net. It does not solve shared-return voltage or noise.
Neck
A short, narrower copper segment used to preserve clearance around a pad or component.
Mask bridge
A solder-mask web or clearance finding between nearby copper features.
Typed pair
Two copper objects counted for a specific rule type. Repeated reports can refer to the same pair.
Voltage domain
A group of circuit nodes that can reach a stated voltage range under the listed conditions.
