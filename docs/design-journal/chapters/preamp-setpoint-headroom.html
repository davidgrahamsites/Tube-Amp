Preamp setpoint large-signal comparison

November 26, 2026 · HD-010 · Paired 1 kHz model screen

Question and test conditions

HD-010 compares the earlier 273.41 V preamp setpoint with the 267.11 V candidate. It runs 16 cases: both setpoints, three input amplitudes per channel, two low-rail cases, and two finer-timestep repeats.

All runs use a 1 kHz input, an 8 Ω modeled load, one knob setting per channel, volume at 1, and tone and gain at 0.5. The model uses generic tubes and allocated reciprocal-transformer models. B_MAIN is stiff at 400 V, B_SCREEN at 380 V, and B_PRE is a fixed DC source. Supply and protection dynamics are absent.

The 18 mV clean case and 1.26 mV dirty case are overdriven. Their roughly 32.6 W outputs have about 22% distortion. They are not clean-output ratings. The 9 mV and 0.63 mV cases produce about 24 W fundamental with 5.6–5.8% distortion. None of these watt values is a clean 25 W amplifier rating.

Low-rail and timestep checks

At the 251.8806 V low-profile rail, the clean case produces 23.3912 W at 5.3645% THD. The dirty case produces 23.1210 W at 5.2529% THD.

The nominal 267.11 V clean case repeats at half the maximum timestep. Fundamental power changes from 24.1975 W to 24.1907 W, or −0.0281%. The dirty case changes from 24.0699 W to 24.0621 W, or −0.0324%. Both are below 0.033%. This checks timestep sensitivity for these two waveforms. It does not prove model accuracy or supply dynamics.

Scope and decision

The saved waveforms settled within the checker tolerance. The independent check reports 205 passing checks for the stated paired comparison. Fundamental watts exclude harmonic power; THD is reported separately.

Carry 267.11 V as the candidate setpoint from HD-009. HD-010 compares simulated audio at two fixed supply values. It does not establish regulator behavior, clipping recovery, actual transformer response, physical output power, or a clean 25 W rating. No circuit change or physical qualification is recorded here.

Vocabulary

Preamp setpoint
The fixed DC voltage used for the preamp supply in each model case.
Fundamental power
Output power at the 1 kHz test frequency, excluding harmonic power.
Total harmonic distortion (THD)
Harmonic output relative to the fundamental. This report sums harmonics 2 through 10.
Stiff supply
A modeled rail that holds its voltage instead of sagging under load.
Fixed DC source
A supply model held at one voltage. It does not include regulator response.
Overdrive
An input level that drives the modeled signal path into substantial nonlinear distortion.
Timestep refinement
A repeat with smaller simulation time steps to check numerical sensitivity.
Settling
The reduction of waveform change between the final ten-cycle window and the preceding ten-cycle window.
