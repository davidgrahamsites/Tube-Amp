Pot harness DC fault screen

November 26, 2026 · HI-006 · Conditional electrical screen; no part approval

Why the harness needs a DC review

Several panel potentiometers connect through coupling capacitors to tube-stage nodes. A shorted capacitor can put DC on a pot and its harness. Normal blocking does not prove a low-voltage fault condition. A connector voltage rating also cannot protect a lower-rated pot or insulation.

HI-006 checks 1,050 normalized linear DC cases, seven analytic or rejection controls, and ten capacitor-short scenarios: C2, C3, C4, C8, C15, C16, C17, C19, C21, and C606. Each network uses source-derived resistor values and exact zero-ohm fault endpoints. Wiper settings are electrical resistance fractions 0, 0.5, and 1. They are not shaft angles or audio-taper positions.

Model boundary and assumptions

The 1,050-case screen treats each network as a linear DC circuit. It opens intact capacitors at DC and omits capacitor leakage, nonlinear tube behavior, and signal transients. It does not find actual maximum voltages or prove full single-fault safety. Its values depend on stated source assumptions and operating states.

  • Clean and dirty-gain screens use a cold, open first tube and retain the actual feed resistors. They omit later grid current. The inactive channel grounds its second-grid node.
  • The dirty-tone screen uses an ideal fixed cathode-follower output voltage and retains R23. The normalled effects path is active; C607 blocks wet-output DC.
  • The C19 case drives the phase-inverter tail through R25 and sends DC backward through the native normalled effects path. A future buffered effects path needs a new review.
  • The presence case drives through R28 from a fixed phase-inverter tail. It is not a direct B+ feed. The reverb case uses a fixed recovery output and an unloaded wiper. It omits real op-amp current limit, mute, and summing-input behavior.

These choices preserve the existing functional blocks. They do not approve a connector, pot, wire, insulation system, or circuit change.

Normalized calculation

For each setting and fault, the network reports a terminal coefficient \(\alpha\) and total track-power coefficient \(\beta\). The source voltage \(V_s\) is then applied as a stated scenario:

\[|V_{terminal}|=\alpha|V_s|,\qquad P_{track}=\beta V_s^2\]

For a comparison power \(P_c\), the source voltage at that comparison is:

\[V_{s,c}=\sqrt{\frac{P_c}{\beta}}\]

Example: C8 short, first tube open, and RV4 wiper at the grounded end. The actual R12 feed and 1 MΩ pot produce:

\[V_{RV4,1}=V_{B\_EARLY}\frac{1\;\mathrm{M\Omega}}{100\;\mathrm{k\Omega}+1\;\mathrm{M\Omega}}=0.909091V_{B\_EARLY}\]

At an assumed 300 V B_EARLY source, \(0.909091\times300\;\mathrm V=272.727\;\mathrm V\). This is a conditional arithmetic example. It is not a measured source voltage or a qualified supply maximum.

Loaded-tube follow-up

A second screen ran 12 paired generic-tube DC cases. It used three C15, C16, and C17 settings, each intact and faulted, at fixed B_PRE values of 267.11 V and 355 V. It uses generic tube models and the desired buffered-loop fixture, not the native normalled-path linear screen. Pot endpoints use a 1 Ω approximation; a failed capacitor uses 1 mΩ. The model tubes are not exact production tubes. No actual supply dynamics or hardware were tested.

For C17, loaded DR_CF is 148.603 V and RV7 total-track power is 0.162057 W at 267.11 V B_PRE. At 355 V B_PRE, DR_CF is 198.763 V and RV7 power is 0.289923 W. The second result is above the 0.25 W A1M comparison. Do not call either result a pass or failure. The exact linear RV7 rating is unknown. Other Alpha family sheets may specify different limits. Actual tube variation, supply behavior, hot operation, and wiper or partial-track stress remain open. Do not redesign solely because this conditional comparison exceeds 0.25 W.

The earlier 300 V stiff-source screen gave 0.660469 W for C17/RV7. The loaded-tube fixture gives lower values under a different source and loop model. These results are not interchangeable acceptance tests. Their difference shows why the actual follower impedance and operating state matter.

Pot rating and decision

The Alpha RV24AF family sheet lists 250 V AC maximum operation. This does not establish a 250 V DC working rating for the exact suffix. The 500 V AC, one-minute dielectric test is an insulation test, not a continuous working limit. Exact DC, total-track, partial-track, and wiper limits remain unresolved.

Keep the current pot values and functional blocks. Do not approve lower-voltage wire from normal DC conditions. Do not treat a high-voltage connector as protection for the pot. Do not add a blocking component without reviewing response, fault behavior, and fit. No native change or connector, pot, or insulation approval was made.

The result receipt passes seven analytic and rejection controls. Maximum KCL residual is \(1.02\times10^{-20}\;\mathrm{A/V}\); maximum power-balance error is \(1.36\times10^{-20}\;\mathrm{W/V^2}\). These checks support the linear calculation within its declared scope. They do not verify source maxima, full single-fault safety, or physical parts.

Next checks

Use selected supply and tube models to bound B_EARLY, DR_CF, PI_TAIL_TOP, and REC_OUT. Obtain exact pot DC, track-power, partial-track, and wiper limits. Include local heating and insulation. Recheck C19 with the future buffered loop. Preserve block ownership and functions while reviewing any evidence-based correction.

Vocabulary

DC
A steady voltage or current that does not alternate polarity.
AC
Alternating current, whose polarity changes with time.
Coupling capacitor
A capacitor that passes changing signals and normally blocks steady DC.
Potentiometer
A variable resistor with a moving contact called a wiper.
Track
The resistive element inside a potentiometer.
Wiper
The moving contact on the potentiometer track.
Normalized coefficient
A result calculated for a unit source, then scaled to a stated source voltage.
Stiff source
A model source that holds voltage when the load changes.
Source impedance
The resistance and reactance that limit current from a source.
Normalled path
The jack path used when no external plug interrupts its contacts.
Phase inverter
The amplifier stage that drives the output tubes with opposite signals.
Cathode follower
A tube stage whose output follows its input voltage and can supply load current.
Dielectric test
A specified insulation withstand test; it does not set continuous working voltage.
Working voltage
The continuous operating voltage allowed for a part under stated conditions.
A1M
An audio-taper potentiometer value code for 1 MΩ.
B25K
A linear-taper potentiometer value code for 25 kΩ.
Grid current
Current flowing into a tube control grid.
KCL
Kirchhoff's current law: current entering a node balances current leaving it.
B+ / B_EARLY
The high-voltage DC supply; B_EARLY is the named supply node used in this screen.
PI_TAIL_TOP
The upper node of the phase-inverter tail network.
DR_CF
The dirty-channel cathode-follower output node used as the modeled source boundary.
REC_OUT
The reverb recovery-stage output node used in the screen.
