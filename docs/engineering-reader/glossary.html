Engineering vocabulary key

Definitions used in the current amplifier reports. Component references and net names retain their exact spelling.

AC
Alternating current; current that changes direction.
B+
The positive high-voltage supply used by the tube circuits.
B_MAIN
The supply node that feeds the output-transformer centre tap.
B_RAW
The rectified supply node before the standby switch.
B_SCREEN
The filtered supply node for the output-tube screen grids.
B_PRE
The filtered supply node for the specified preamplifier stages.
B_EARLY
The filtered supply node for the earlier preamplifier stages.
Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Bleeder
A resistor used to remove capacitor charge; its presence alone does not prove safe residual voltage.
Brownout
A supply-voltage reduction below the normal operating range.
Bypass capacitor
A capacitor that provides a short local path for changing current.
Capacitance
The ratio of stored charge to voltage; measured in farads (F).
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Cassette
A removable assembly carrying the electronics and its supports.
Chassis
The metal structure that supports and encloses the electronics.
Chuck
The drill part that grips the bit.
Clearance
The shortest distance through air between conductive parts.
Common-mode voltage
Voltage shared by conductors relative to a specified reference.
Creepage
The shortest path along an insulating surface between conductive parts.
CT
Centre tap; an electrical connection part-way along a transformer winding.
Datum
A fixed point, line or surface used as the measurement reference.
DC
Direct current; current that flows in one direction.
Derating
Operating below a component rating to allow for heat or other specified conditions.
Dissipation
Electrical energy converted to heat per unit time; measured in watts (W).
DRC
Design Rule Check; a PCB geometry and connectivity check.
EMI
Electromagnetic interference; unwanted electrical or magnetic coupling.
Envelope
The space occupied by a part or assembly, including stated allowances.
ERC
Electrical Rules Check; a schematic consistency check.
Faraday shield
A conductive shield used against electric-field coupling; it does not guarantee low-frequency magnetic shielding.
Feedback
A signal returned from an output to an earlier stage to control gain or response.
Flange
A projecting edge used to attach or stiffen a part.
Functional block
A group of parts with one defined job and specified connections to other groups.
GB1
The design identifier for the declared signal-ground to chassis connection.
Ground
A named circuit reference; it is not automatically protective earth.
Ground loop
A closed conducting path between ground connections that can carry unwanted current.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
Headroom
The signal margin before clipping or another specified limit.
Heater
The tube element that heats the cathode.
Heater reference
The voltage used to set the heater circuit relative to the tube cathodes.
Heater-to-cathode voltage
The voltage between a tube heater and cathode; it must stay within the tube limits.
HREF
The design name for the heater reference node.
HV
High voltage; use the actual voltage and insulation requirement in each design check.
ICM
Interpretable Context Methodology; the project structure that records ownership, inputs, outputs and checks.
Impedance
Opposition to alternating current, including resistance and frequency-dependent effects; measured in ohms (Ω).
Inrush
The brief current rise when power is applied.
KaTeX
The software used to display mathematical notation in these HTML documents.
Lead
A component terminal that connects to the board.
Lead protrusion
The length of a component lead below the board.
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Loop resistance
The total resistance of the supply and return conductors, contacts and terminations.
Magnetics
Transformers and inductors.
Mains
The building AC power supply.
MOQ
Minimum order quantity.
Net
A named set of electrically connected points.
Node
An electrical connection shared by specified components.
Nominal
The stated reference value before tolerance and operating variation.
PCB
Printed circuit board.
PE
Protective earth; the protective connection to exposed conductive parts.
Pin map
A table assigning each connector contact to a circuit node and function.
Populated
With the components installed.
Preamp
Preamplifier; the stages that prepare the input signal for the power amplifier.
Qualification
Checks that show a design meets stated limits under stated conditions.
RC time constant
Resistance multiplied by capacitance for a simple resistor-capacitor model; not a safe service delay.
Rectifier
A circuit that changes alternating current into current of one polarity.
Reservoir
A capacitor bank that stores energy after rectification.
Return path
The route that carries current back to its source.
RFI
Radio-frequency interference.
Ripple
Periodic supply variation remaining after rectification and filtering.
RLC
Resistance, inductance and capacitance.
Screen grid
An electrode in the output tube supplied from the screen supply.
Schematic
A drawing of electrical connections and component functions.
Series
Connected in sequence so the same branch current passes through each part.
Parallel
Connected across the same two electrical nodes.
Skin effect
Current crowding near a conductor surface as frequency increases.
Socket
A replaceable connection that holds a tube or integrated circuit.
Standby
A switch state; it does not by itself prove the amplifier is de-energized.
Strain relief
A support that keeps cable forces away from electrical terminations.
Swarf
Metal chips left by drilling or cutting.
Tank
The mechanical spring-reverb assembly.
Tolerance
The permitted variation from a specified value.
Transformer
A magnetic device that transfers energy between windings.
Winding
A coil of insulated wire in a transformer or inductor.
Wet/dry
The balance between the effect signal and the unaffected signal.
K1
The candidate with the bulk and screen supply below and stage filters above.
K2
The upper power-assembly reference layout; it is not yet a proven fit.
K4
The candidate with all power-supply filters below.
Gate
A checkpoint that requires specified evidence before the next activity.
Prototype
A test assembly used to check the design before release.
Residual voltage
Voltage still present after power removal or disconnection.
Stored energy
Energy held in a charged capacitor; it depends on capacitance and voltage.
Dummy load
A resistor assembly that absorbs amplifier output power in place of a speaker during electrical tests.
Sense resistor
A known resistor used to calculate current from its measured voltage drop.
Oscilloscope
An instrument that displays voltage against time.
Differential probe
A probe that measures voltage between two points within its specified differential and earth-referenced voltage limits.
Attenuator
A circuit that reduces a signal to a specified level.
Clipping
Signal flattening when the amplifier reaches its output limit.
Parasitic
An unintended electrical effect caused by physical conductors, parts or layout.
Thermal
Related to heat or temperature.
Insulation resistance
Resistance through an insulating path, measured at a specified test voltage.
Protective bond
The intentional protective-earth connection to exposed conductive metal.
Frequency response
How gain and phase change with signal frequency.
Impedance sweep
An impedance measurement repeated across a specified range of frequencies.
Cathode
The tube electrode that emits electrons when heated.
Cathode follower
A tube stage that takes its signal output from the cathode.
Divider
Series components that produce a fraction of an applied voltage.
THD
Total harmonic distortion; harmonic content relative to the fundamental, using the stated harmonic range.
Fundamental
The waveform component at the applied signal frequency.
Timestep
The time interval used by the simulator to resolve a waveform.
Transient
A change in circuit conditions over time, such as startup or shutdown.
Stiff supply
A modeled voltage source whose voltage does not change with load.
Corner
A selected combination of component and operating limits.
