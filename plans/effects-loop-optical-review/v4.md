# Effects-loop and optical-control implementation plan

Objective: qualify the existing J3 SEND/J4 normalled RETURN in the 220V electric Renegade amplifier. Preserve its six tube stages/devices, channel behavior, constant-dry reverb control, functional blocks, connectorized insulated harnesses and received cabinet. Do not add an input buffer, change the acoustic branch, add a tube or relocate the loop without an explicit architecture decision. This document is a plan, not a fabrication release.

## Recommendation and evidence
Retain K1/K2/K3 and their MOSFET coil drivers as the baseline. Optocoupler→MOSFET→relay is useful for a separate control domain, but our shared supply/ground defeats system galvanic isolation. It retains coil demand, contact motion and audio switching transients. It does not cure an audio ground loop. Only investigate an optical substitute after identifying a measured benefit; K3 wet-only muting is the first candidate. Preserve its de-energized mute. Ordinary phototransistor optocouplers are control devices, not bilateral audio contacts. PhotoMOS requires voltage, off-capacitance, leakage and default-state checks; one SPST is not a DPDT replacement. [Omron](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g5v2.pdf), [Panasonic terminology](https://industry.panasonic.com/global/en/products/control/relay/photomos/term).

K2 selects CLEAN_OUT/DIRTY_OUT→FX_SEND. J4 normals to FX_SEND; its return feeds both spring send and passive dry mixing before the PI. Clean source is plate-coupled; dirty source is a master-pot wiper. R100 DNP leaves an indirect ~10.1MΩ DC reference through R612/R24. Lowering return impedance can change dry gain and wet injection. Historical isolated-preamp results include ~11.95V peak clean; they are generic-model,1MΩ-load results, not integrated measurements. Do not assume a ±15V buffer can accept or restore the native signal. [TI OPA2134](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/opa2134.pdf).

## Dependency-ordered work
1. **Freeze the comparison circuit and coverage — 2–4 hours.** Record file hashes, component populations and all loop/spring/PI nets. Model actual bootstrapped PI loading. Cover both channels, full usable gain/master ranges, tone extremes,82Hz/1kHz/5kHz signals, pick-like bursts and clipping recovery; include the existing240/278/320V sensitivity cases without treating them as qualified supply limits. Record positive/negative peaks, DC offsets and source impedance. Establish required PI drive and supply corners. Done: repeatable baseline fixture and coverage table; unresolved model behavior is marked, not a passing result.

2. **Set interface and physical limits — 2–3 hours, after baseline.** Define a bounded pedal compatibility envelope, send attenuation, return gain and permissible overdrive. DD200 is a documented example, not the user's selected pedal: nominal−10dBu, maximum+7dBu; a1.23Vpeak SEND ceiling provides6dB below that maximum. State actual peak limits, not RMS assumptions for distorted waveforms. [BOSS](https://www.boss.info/fr/products/dd-200/specifications/). Proposed electrical targets: SEND≤1kΩ80Hz–10kHz into100kΩ||2nF and1MΩ||2nF; RETURN≥1MΩmidband from1kΩ/10kΩ sources; unity-equivalent response±0.5dB20Hz–20kHz; added THD+N<0.1%1kHz within the qualified envelope. Independently compare dry-to-PI, spring drive and wet-to-PI gains: aim≤0.5dB change80Hz–5kHz across intended controls where a transfer is nonzero; define absolute residual limits at mute/null settings. Any relaxed tolerance or reduced native envelope must be declared before acceptance. Screen jack bodies, plugs, cable bends and service paths in the actual cabinet. Done: numerical limits, coverage and preliminary packaging; tool dimensions remain required for final drill-access signoff.

3. **Choose recovery and bypass — 2–4 hours, after limits.** Compare passive baseline, low-voltage buffered interface and discrete high-voltage recovery at the existing insertion point. Select±15V only if input common mode, guaranteed loaded output swing and rail/temperature margins cover every required case. Attenuation alone cannot restore lost headroom. HV loop precedents are not Renegade drop-ins: [designer tube loop](https://www.valvewizard.co.uk/fxlooppcb.html), [manufacturer discrete loop](https://mojotone.com/products/mojotone-discrete-hi-voltage-series-effects-loop-for-vacuum-tube-amplifiers). Compare buffered bypass with restoration of the original passive path. Separate automatic power-loss behavior from manual recovery after component failure; test loading by both send and return branches. Done: selected topology with explicit preserved behavior, bypass/fault matrix, supply budget and no unresolved signal-ceiling contradiction.

4. **Engineer protection, parts and fixture — 4–8 hours plus sourcing.** Bound each normal/abnormal voltage, source impedance and duration, including tube coupling-capacitor leakage/short, powered/unpowered external pedal, supply loss and clamp back-feeding. Define accessible-port safety and component-stress acceptance from the actual insulation architecture and applicable requirements before energizing. Give capacitor sides and disconnected throws deliberate DC references; include loading, resistor working voltage/power, clamp energy and discharge time. Do not rely on internal ESD diodes as the port barrier. [TI protection](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/spva018/spva018.pdf), [ADI back-powering](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/app-notes/an-1387.html). Verify exact parts, packages, pin/slot geometry and mainland low-quantity supply. Indexed global USD prices are not verified domestic RMB inventory. Done: readable candidate schematic/BOM, protection calculations and independent interface simulation/prototype; no unsupported optical substitution.

5. **Integrate the supported design — 4–8 hours, after fixture checks.** Keep audio, power, reverb and control ownership explicit within the preferred three physical PCBs. Add local bypass/filtering, controlled coil returns, short sensitive paths and connectorized insulated cables. Keep protective earth independent of signal-ground experiments. Fit fixed-structure chassis mounts, accessible screws and independently removable wooden rear cover. Check actual drill approach/withdrawal, tube service paths and installed plugs. Update native schematic, BOM, footprints and routing; verify pin/net parity, ERC/DRC, current widths and insulation clearances. Done: checked native artifacts and populated assembly evidence; absent measurements mean HOLD.

6. **Qualify and record — 4–8 hours plus bench access, after integration.** Test no cables, send-only, return-only, both, partial insertion, open/short cables, unpowered pedals, startup/shutdown/brownout and control-power loss. Send-only shorts must not damage the driver or interrupt dry audio if that behavior is specified. Open external returns need not auto-bypass. Require integrated dry/spring/wet transfer, headroom, loaded supply, thermal, hum/noise and switching checks; standalone success is insufficient. Normal settled portDC target≤10mV. Define fixed nodes and loads for noise/transients before testing: initially normalized return output at the approved recovery gain,1kΩ termination,20Hz–20kHz unweighted noise≤−85dBu referred to return input; zero-input switching transient≤10mVpeak at a separately defined low-level monitor with stated attenuation/bandwidth. Also evaluate live-signal switching and speaker audibility. These are proposed engineering targets, not standards. Done: logs with measured/modelled status, failures resolved or release held, ICM decision/status and catalogs updated, reviewed changes committed and pushed to the electric branch without including unrelated work.

## Remaining gates
Full-amp model validity, required return swing, acceptable all-setting reverb changes, actual pedal envelope, component protection, mainland supply, populated cabinet fit and actual drill envelope remain unresolved. No circuit/PCB change is made by this planning review. Effort estimates are hands-on engineering time, not delivery promises; procurement and physical bench access are additional. Next action: Step1 baseline/interface qualification, not ordering an optical relay or drilling a panel.
