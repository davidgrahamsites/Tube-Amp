# Renegade five-unit combo build plan

## Scope and assumptions
Develop a KiCad 10 design for a 220 V / 50 Hz tube guitar combo with built-in speaker, tube-driven spring reverb, debounced channel/reverb footswitch, sleek front panel and printable cabinet graphics. Source and deliver in Shanghai. Use free design software and reproducible automation wherever supported.

The user has some build experience and substantial test-equipment experience; retain written build/test guidelines. Original amplifier PPTX, output power, original tube count, budget, exact equipment remain unresolved. Sale of all five is being evaluated; commercial release is conditional on the separate feasibility, conformity and demand gates below. The existing KiCad package is only a three-symbol preview. Never substitute an unrelated amplifier for the missing source.

Plan five accepted finished units, with one prototype becoming unit one after any required retrofit, then four repeats. If it cannot be economically accepted, budget a replacement explicitly. For costing only, assume one 12-inch speaker, two output tubes plus three original small-signal tubes, and added 12AT7/12AX7 reverb. These are hypothetical counts, not verified circuit facts.

## Brainstorm decisions and economical starting direction
Compare one large PCB, modular boards and a hybrid of PCBs with chassis-mounted heavy/hot parts. Start by evaluating the hybrid, then choose against wiring/noise, heat, MOQ and assembly cost. The user prefers the Mable 5E3 stock combo shell. Keep it as the first candidate, contingent on real inner dimensions/chassis opening and tank/speaker/heat/service fit; compare custom CNC plywood and locally assembled flat parts if it fails.

Explore three visual concepts: matte black/ivory with one accent; brushed metal/black; and off-white with restrained graphics. Prefer standard covering or matte paint and a small printed badge/decal in the base budget. Compare drilled aluminum with digital adhesive overlay, direct UV print and engraving; include setup charges before choosing screen printing. Keep controls grouped and readable; actual labels/counts follow the recovered source.

## Fabrication, construction and review rules
Maintain one canonical interface.json: millimetres, origins/viewing side, coordinate transforms, part envelopes, controls/labels, speaker pattern/depth, board/chassis/panel/cabinet holes/slots, connectors and revision. Generate downstream CAD/artwork where supported and fail release if interfaces/revisions disagree. Native schematic connectivity remains authoritative. An independent reviewer checks critical dimensions, pin maps and coordinate transforms against drawings/measured samples and calibrated full-size templates before fabrication; matching generated files alone cannot validate the register's inputs.

Determine electrical spacing from working/startup/no-load voltages, transients, insulation function, environmental/material conditions and the applicable product requirements; size copper from current, thickness and temperature conditions. No universal tube-PCB safe spacing/trace number is supplied. [TI insulation guide](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sdaa268/sdaa268.pdf), [IPC-2152 scope](https://www.ipc.org/TOC/IPC-2152.pdf).

Retain internally reviewed plated/nonplated drill files and a finished-hole schedule; deliver only the chosen fabricator's agreed package. KiCad defaults and JLC instructions differ on merged/separate drills and oval encoding. Check drill-to-pad alignment, continuous outline, cutouts, slots, finished diameters and CAM interpretation. Boards arrive fabricated/drilled as specified; metal panels, chassis and wooden cabinets need separate machining drawings/orders. [KiCad PCB manual](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html), [JLC export guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-generate-gerber-and-drill-files-in-kicad-9).

Build guidelines cover protective earth, mains protection, insulation/accessibility, discharge, strain relief, rated components, wiring/grounding and safe bench setup. Identify the competent reviewer, rates, availability and equipment at T02; do not defer this until a finished prototype exists. Before energizing, that reviewer checks the construction. Confirm equipment, rated load/probes and written acceptance limits for power, operating points, hum/noise, heat, reverb, reset/unplugging/pops and mechanical loads. CAD/ERC/DRC/simulation do not prove these measured properties. Record incoming lot/part identities, approved tube operating/bias limits and build/harness consistency; test every repeat unit and budget replacements/spares for variation.

Artwork retains editable masters and outlined production copies; agree printer color/white-ink, bleed and material requirements. Proof actual size and washer/knob coverage; test ink/adhesive on the selected finish. For possible sales, classify the finished amplifier and supplied accessories before assuming CCC costs. Official CNCA sources removed audio amplifiers and the specified active-speaker category from CCC in 2022; removal does not eliminate safety/quality/labeling/service duties. GB 4943.1-2022 is a current mandatory safety standard whose product applicability/tests need competent review. Identify applicable EMC, RoHS and consumer-service requirements and fund any testing, manual/labels, repairs/returns and traceability. Small batch size is not a substitute for classification. [CNCA removal](https://www.cnca.gov.cn/zwxx/gg/zjgg/art/2022/art_08ab3d8ec62842959fe3c602db6113b5.html), [safety standard](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=862145EBF14F5CC3771FC64CE94A48DF), [Product Quality Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zfjcj/tzgg/art/2023/art_579118cd202a45fba28b7edfd9f6fd72.html).

Check rights to commercialize the recovered source and clear the production name/logo before printing; the project working name and supplier-style cabinet are not a branding licence.
