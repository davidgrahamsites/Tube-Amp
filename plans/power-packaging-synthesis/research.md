T2.CT branches from **B_MAIN**, before L1; it must receive its own defined feed/return arrangement when T2 stays upper. Hybrid B_SCREEN also supplies the upper R35 network and output screen resistors. Include lower R501/R502 with C29/C30 and the transformer CT return in the raw-reservoir allocation; otherwise the shorthand component list leaves its discharge/charging-loop ownership incomplete. R35/R17 are each 10 kΩ. C12=B_EARLY/GND, C13 and C25 are separate 50 µF B_PRE/GND parts, and C26=B_SCREEN/GND: v2 has these correctly. [Current circuit](../../engineering/whole-amp/reverb-600/circuit.json), [prior interface table](../bottom-power-enclosure/harness-research.md).

2. **Turn the deferred power seam into a named decision receipt before harness quotation.** Record S1's two actual paths (`L_FUSED/L_SW` and `MAINS_N/N_SW`) and S2's `B_RAW/B_MAIN` path; an upper S2 can create a round trip to lower L1 even with upper T2. Both native mains transformers T1 **and T3** need the selectable-mains implementation. “Selected mains/auxiliary power magnetics” should read “exact magnetics to be selected/qualified”: current native references do not establish selected dual-voltage parts. Name ownership of R40/D7/C28/R39/C27/R38/RV10/R503, the bias adjustment and BIAS_OUT/open-return failure. For auxiliary allocation, distinguish raw `CTRL_RAW/NEG_RAW` crossings from regulated `+15V/-15V/+12V/+5V` crossings; preserve regulator-local capacitors/protection and load-end reset/mute behavior. Do not infer connector count from this candidate list. [Circuit](../../engineering/whole-amp/reverb-600/circuit.json), [harness research](../bottom-power-enclosure/harness-research.md), [mains decision](../../brain/records/DEC-015-switchable-mains.md).

3. **Keep heater and charge ownership coupled.** R506/R507/C505 currently connect B_SCREEN/HREF/GND. Their physical side affects both heater common-mode/reference behavior and downstream discharge after unplugging. Separate heater groups remain a candidate, and do not by themselves solve the 355 V preamp case; one continuous winding's center tap is not independent isolation. Preserve this as a transformer/harness blocker while allowing inert geometry work. Explicitly inspect the received tank's grounded output shell/mounts for a second GND/chassis bond in the new metal assembly. [HD-002](../../engineering/component-selection/heater-divider-rating/interface-review.md), [grounding contract](../../engineering/whole-amp/reverb-600/grounding-contract.json), [fit register](../../engineering/whole-amp/mechanical/fit-register.json).

4. **Restore the strict P2 selection/evidence conditions.** A passive K1/K4 filter comparison is useful only if both arrangements survive P1, K4 has meaningful whole-assembly gain, magnetic uncertainty is already addressed, and a source/load/cable sensitivity estimate predicts a resolvable decision-relevant effect. Freeze actual R/L/C, source impedance, incremental load representation, bandwidth and absolute limits. Use three repeated traces plus repeated reference, with common exact parts; disclose unavoidable mismatches and mark the comparison inconclusive if they could reverse the result. Low-voltage terminations omit operating-voltage, active-load and nonlinear effects; if those omissions matter, label the test exploratory. Replacing P2 with magnetic testing leaves filter performance open, and vice versa. A fallback consumes a replacement slot, not an extra protocol.
