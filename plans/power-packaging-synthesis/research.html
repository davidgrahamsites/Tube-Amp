  1. Make the remaining HV seam explicit. The source topology is B_RAW → S2 → B_MAIN → L1 → B_SCREEN → R35 → B_PRE → R17 → B_EARLY. T2.CT branches from B_MAIN, before L1; it must receive its own defined feed/return arrangement when T2 stays upper. Hybrid B_SCREEN also supplies the upper R35 network and output screen resistors.   2. Turn the deferred power seam into a named decision receipt before harness quotation. Record S1's two actual paths (L_FUSED/L_SW and MAINS_N/N_SW) and S2's B_RAW/B_MAIN path; an upper S2 can create a round trip to lower L1 even with upper T2. Both native mains transformers T1 and T3 need the selectable-mains implementation. “Selected mains/auxiliary power magnetics” should read “exact magnetics to be selected/qualified”: current native references do not establish selected dual-voltage parts. Name ownership of R40/D7/C28/R39/C27/R38/RV10/R503, the bias adjustment and BIAS_OUT/open-return failure. For auxiliary allocation, distinguish raw CTRL_RAW/NEG_RAW crossings from regulated +15V/-15V/+12V/+5V crossings; preserve regulator-local capacitors/protection and load-end reset/mute behavior. Do not infer connector count from this candidate list. Circuit, harness research, mains decision.

  3. Keep heater and charge ownership coupled. R506/R507/C505 currently connect B_SCREEN/HREF/GND. Their physical side affects both heater common-mode/reference behavior and downstream discharge after unplugging. Separate heater groups remain a candidate, and do not by themselves solve the 355 V preamp case; one continuous winding's center tap is not independent isolation. Preserve this as a transformer/harness blocker while allowing inert geometry work. Explicitly inspect the received tank's grounded output shell/mounts for a second GND/chassis bond in the new metal assembly. HD-002, grounding contract, fit register.

  4. Restore the strict P2 selection/evidence conditions. A passive K1/K4 filter comparison is useful only if both arrangements survive P1, K4 has meaningful whole-assembly gain, magnetic uncertainty is already addressed, and a source/load/cable sensitivity estimate predicts a resolvable decision-relevant effect. Freeze actual R/L/C, source impedance, incremental load representation, bandwidth and absolute limits. Use three repeated traces plus repeated reference, with common exact parts; disclose unavoidable mismatches and mark the comparison inconclusive if they could reverse the result. Low-voltage terminations omit operating-voltage, active-load and nonlinear effects; if those omissions matter, label the test exploratory. Replacing P2 with magnetic testing leaves filter performance open, and vice versa. A fallback consumes a replacement slot, not an extra protocol. Alien final protocols and decision tree.

Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

B_MAIN
The supply node that feeds the output-transformer centre tap.
B_RAW
The rectified supply node before the standby switch.
B_SCREEN
The filtered supply node for the output-tube screen grids.
B_PRE
The filtered supply node for the specified preamplifier stages.
B_EARLY
The filtered supply node for the earlier preamplifier stages.
Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Chassis
The metal structure that supports and encloses the electronics.
Clearance
The shortest distance through air between conductive parts.
CT
Centre tap; an electrical connection part-way along a transformer winding.
Envelope
The space occupied by a part or assembly, including stated allowances.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
Heater
The tube element that heats the cathode.
HREF
The design name for the heater reference node.
HV
High voltage; use the actual voltage and insulation requirement in each design check.
ICM
Interpretable Context Methodology; the project structure that records ownership, inputs, outputs and checks.
Impedance
Opposition to alternating current, including resistance and frequency-dependent effects; measured in ohms (Ω).
Lead
A component terminal that connects to the board.
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Magnetics
Transformers and inductors.
Mains
The building AC power supply.
Preamp
Preamplifier; the stages that prepare the input signal for the power amplifier.
Qualification
Checks that show a design meets stated limits under stated conditions.
Reservoir
A capacitor bank that stores energy after rectification.
Tank
The mechanical spring-reverb assembly.
Transformer
A magnetic device that transfers energy between windings.
Winding
A coil of insulated wire in a transformer or inductor.
K1
The candidate with the bulk and screen supply below and stage filters above.
K4
The candidate with all power-supply filters below.
Prototype
A test assembly used to check the design before release.
