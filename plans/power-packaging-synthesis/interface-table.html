Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

B_MAIN
The supply node that feeds the output-transformer centre tap.
B_RAW
The rectified supply node before the standby switch.
B_SCREEN
The filtered supply node for the output-tube screen grids.
B_PRE
The filtered supply node for the specified preamplifier stages.
B_EARLY
The filtered supply node for the earlier preamplifier stages.
Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Chassis
The metal structure that supports and encloses the electronics.
CT
Centre tap; an electrical connection part-way along a transformer winding.
Feedback
A signal returned from an output to an earlier stage to control gain or response.
GB1
The design identifier for the declared signal-ground to chassis connection.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
HV
High voltage; use the actual voltage and insulation requirement in each design check.
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Magnetics
Transformers and inductors.
Mains
The building AC power supply.
PE
Protective earth; the protective connection to exposed conductive parts.
Qualification
Checks that show a design meets stated limits under stated conditions.
Rectifier
A circuit that changes alternating current into current of one polarity.
Reservoir
A capacitor bank that stores energy after rectification.
Schematic
A drawing of electrical connections and component functions.
Transformer
A magnetic device that transfers energy between windings.
Winding
A coil of insulated wire in a transformer or inductor.
