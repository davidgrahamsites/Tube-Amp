# Combined amplifier power-packaging plan

## Decision to resolve
Compare a hybrid lower power module against a repacked upper power assembly, preserving approximately25W, switchable120/220V, the fixed cabinet, functional blocks, sockets, independent rear wood, low cost and real screw access. The provisional first candidate is lower power generation/rectification/raw reservoir plus screen filtering, with early/preamp filters and local bypass near upper loads. This is a research priority, not an adopted architecture or a proven fit. A metal lower enclosure may improve packaging but can worsen tank hum, acoustic response, heat, structural/service burden and harness complexity.

## Frozen electrical allocation for comparison
- Lower hybrid candidate: exact mains/auxiliary power magnetics to be selected and qualified, inlet/selector/fuses/PE provisions, HV rectifier D3–D6/F2, raw reservoir C29/C30 with R501/R502 and transformer CT return, L1 and C26. Exact auxiliary regulation, bias and switching ownership still require a complete interface decision.
- Upper: output transformer T2 initially near output tubes; R35 with C13/C25 on B_PRE; R17 with C12 on B_EARLY; tube/audio/reverb/control functions and their required local bypass. Power-owned components may occupy a reserved upper PCB zone without becoming a new functional block. Preserve values, refs, nodes and deliberate returns.
- All-filter-lower alternative: also moves C12/C13/C25 and associated R17/R35 network below; gains must survive extra rail/return harness and support. C13/C25 share a node but remain separate50uF references. Do not merge capacitance, add unreviewed local parts, or change audio topology for fit.
- Upper-only fallback: repack Power; use a supported capacitor bank only if whole-assembly benefit justifies it. No capacitor suspended on leads. Three physical boards remain the intended architecture; actual native CAD has four incomplete boards.
