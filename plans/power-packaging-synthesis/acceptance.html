Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Chassis
The metal structure that supports and encloses the electronics.
Chuck
The drill part that grips the bit.
Clearance
The shortest distance through air between conductive parts.
Common-mode voltage
Voltage shared by conductors relative to a specified reference.
Creepage
The shortest path along an insulating surface between conductive parts.
Derating
Operating below a component rating to allow for heat or other specified conditions.
Envelope
The space occupied by a part or assembly, including stated allowances.
Feedback
A signal returned from an output to an earlier stage to control gain or response.
GB1
The design identifier for the declared signal-ground to chassis connection.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
Heater
The tube element that heats the cathode.
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Magnetics
Transformers and inductors.
Mains
The building AC power supply.
MOQ
Minimum order quantity.
PE
Protective earth; the protective connection to exposed conductive parts.
Populated
With the components installed.
Tank
The mechanical spring-reverb assembly.
Tolerance
The permitted variation from a specified value.
Transformer
A magnetic device that transfers energy between windings.
Gate
A checkpoint that requires specified evidence before the next activity.
Prototype
A test assembly used to check the design before release.
