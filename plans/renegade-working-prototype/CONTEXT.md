# Working prototype plan

One job: plan one functioning Renegade combo that fits the chosen cabinet.

## Inputs
[Canonical requirements](../../Renegade_Codex_Handoff.md), [project status](../../PROJECT_STATUS.md), received original schematic, and [preferred-shell evidence](../../brain/records/CMP-001-mable-5e3-shell.md). The former five-unit plan supplies research leads, not active production gates.

## Process
Use [plan.md](plan.md) as the public planning seam, with [tasks.md](tasks.md) owning the ordered execution queue and owner-input list. Resolve source and geometry first; coordinate electrical and physical design through a shared interface register. Use the task-card template in `brain/_templates/` when dispatching work. Capture resulting evidence in the brain.

## Outputs
A dependency-ordered prototype plan, explicit unknowns and acceptance gates. Actual engineering outputs belong in their task folders when work begins.

## Human check
Review the cabinet fit evidence, quantity-one spending proposal and electrical review before irreversible manufacture or energizing.
