# Alien Method framing and predeclared rubric

Decision: which physical distribution of power generation, filtering and upper tube/audio circuitry gives a demonstrable whole-cabinet improvement over the current upper-power concept? Preserve electrical functions, fixed delivered cabinet, tank orientation, roughly25W, selectable120/220V, sockets, independent rear cover, insulated connectorized wiring, practical tools and economical China sourcing.

This is a small qualitative adaptation of [The Alien Space of Science](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01092v3), whose primary paper was opened November26. It does not reproduce learned coherence/availability models, establish worldwide novelty or claim superiority over ordinary engineering. The mechanism vocabulary is limited to the cited manufacturer and project sources.

## Rubric declared before candidate generation
Disqualifying gates: altered unapproved circuit behavior; unavailable occupied volume; unsupported heavy parts; unqualified insulation/PE or service-charge state; blocked cover/module/socket/tool service; incompatible tank orientation. Unknown evidence remains conditional, never a pass. Advance only if no known hard failure and a concrete measurable improvement hypothesis; revise if one bounded interface/placement change can address the failure; stop/defer if improvement needs a broad amp redesign or unresolved critical evidence prevents meaningful comparison.

After gates, assess separately: mechanism fit and source strength; implementation prerequisites/cost/reversibility; user value in space, noise and service; familiarity only within reviewed sources. No opaque numerical aggregate or novelty bonus. Retain at most two distinct approaches for bounded protocols; retain ties explicitly.

## Balance and budget
Three conventional and three recombined candidate cards, drawn from the same mechanism catalogue, with identical fields and equal desk-writing allowance. Recombined cards join only two or three compatible mechanisms. Freeze six cards before comparison; show reviewers neutral IDs in a mixed order, while method membership is retained separately. Up to two bounded protocols, no purchase, supplier contact, live test or production adoption from this document. Existing design/ICM/Git authorization covers reversible local research only.
