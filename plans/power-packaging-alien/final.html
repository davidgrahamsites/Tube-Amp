Alien Method power-packaging comparison

Objective and rules

Use source-backed mechanism recombination to test alternatives to the upper-power baseline and proposed bottom enclosure, then select at most two bounded experiments. Preserve the delivered cabinet, approximately25W,120/220V, power/audio/reverb functional blocks, sockets, tank horizontal/open-side-down, independent rear cover, actual tool access and economical China sourcing. No physical design, new circuit or fabrication is adopted. This is qualitative ideation, not paper replication or a novelty claim.

Rubric precedes candidate generation: hard electrical/mechanical/service failures eliminate; unknowns remain conditional. Evaluate mechanism evidence, feasibility/change burden, measurable user value and familiarity separately; no aggregate score or novelty bonus. Three conventional and three recombined cards use the same eight-mechanism catalogue and identical fields/allowance. Membership stays separate from the mixed-ID review order. Frozen cards appear below; do not alter them silently after comparison.

Plan

  1. Audit sources and frozen cards (integration; one session). Trace each mechanism to manufacturer/project evidence and classify each benefit as hypothesis or observation. Verify frozen hash and equal set treatment. Done: no unsupported claim or hidden method preference.
  2. Independently critique and research load-bearing unknowns (review/research; one session each). Examine compatibility of structural support, local filtering, field management, paired harnesses, safe modular service and vertical board trade. Hold exact parts, electrical values and cabinet constraints common. Done: hard-gate matrix and explicit research limits; no missing volume counted as free space.
  3. Compare six cards and select at most two (integration; one session). Record retain/revise/defer/stop per card with reasons and ties. Require concrete whole-assembly gain that survives supports, wiring and service; do not rank local PCB dimensions as cabinet fit. Done: concise shortlist and discarded incompatible combinations, not a selected production architecture.
  4. Write zero, one or at most two justified bounded protocols (integration; one session). P1 compares matched populated geometry and inert service mock-ups, declaring every coupled placement/retention/harness change rather than claiming a pure single-variable experiment. P2 compares electrical/noise consequences with frozen circuit values, actual rated parts and matched loads; live work only in technician-reviewed enclosed fixtures under actual authorization. Set measurement limits/uncertainty before a pass; stop for any hard failure. Done: baseline, changed mechanism, controls, resources, effort cap, observations and stop/go rules for each.
  5. Independent pre-mortem/fresh review, then synthesize (integration; two reviews and one session). Merge accepted edits; record rejected edits with reason. Save ICM/journal/checks/Git. Done: executable protocols and explicit unperformed measurements. Feed results into final combined five-pass review; no claim recombination outperformed ordinary design from this small desk sample.

Eligibility and comparison discipline

Selection means next information to obtain, not proof of superiority. Define fit/service hard criteria and source-backed electrical limits before scoring; missing numeric noise/thermal/cost limits stay unknown. Zero justified experiments is an acceptable outcome. K2 is an unfinished diagnostic baseline, not a safe or build-ready reference; keep its known deficits visible and do not credit an alternative for unrelated electrical fixes.

Use K1/K4 as the cleanest conceptual contrast for local versus remote stage filters, with circuit values fixed and changed lead/return geometry recorded. K2/K6 compares upper packaging, but support/wiring are coupled consequences. K3/K5 are different structural options, not a single-variable matched pair. Separate physical-system comparisons from isolated mechanism tests.

Energized tests require resolved fixture-specific faults, insulation/PE, load ratings, retained charge, bias and heater/reference behavior wherever those circuits are active. An isolated closed power-source/tank-field fixture need not inherit full amplifier operation, but cannot claim its qualification. A low-voltage passive harness fixture cannot establish HV safety or full-amplifier behavior.

Resource caps: at most six desk cards, three placement headings per candidate where applicable, no more than two desk sessions of up to two hours each between checkpoints; intake, research, comparison and drafting all count, and at most two protocol drafts. Stop dependent modeling when missing part/geometry inputs would determine the answer; record owner and next evidence rather than fabricate precision. Use common exact parts and total assembly boundaries; account for both mains modes where relevant to the fixture. Identify unavoidable mismatches before comparison; justify their limited influence or mark the result inconclusive. Include China quantity-one sourcing, MOQ alternatives, custom metal/harness labor and replacement access in feasibility; no unverified delivered totals.

Service criteria are observable: rear cover off/on without electrical disconnection, accessible retaining screws, supported module withdrawal, socket replacement, safe charge verification and reconnection without stressed wires. The shared early geometry gate includes populated parts, connector mating/latches, actual tools, cable corridors, tank and extraction sweeps.

Shared uncertainties

Floor/support and speaker/magnetics geometry; exact capacitor and connector qualification; heater-reference fault still unresolved; bias loss while B+ remains; upper mains/standby control allocation; retained charge and permanent PE on separate modules. Whole-system baseline currently fails dimensional trial and has unfinished routing; it is a comparison reference, not a qualified design.

Frozen comparison cards

Common rubric and source catalogue: method.md and atoms.md. Same fields and three cards per method; displayed order mixes membership. Hypotheses only; no benefit has been measured.

K1 — Lower bulk supply with local stage filtering

  • Hypothesis: Move generation/rectification/reservoir and candidate screen filtering down; retain early/preamp filters with their loads.
  • Mechanisms: M1 + M2 + M5.
  • Benefit over baseline K2: Reclaims upper heavy/bulk space while avoiding long early-stage filtered-node loops.
  • Prerequisites: Floor/tank fit, controlled returns, exact heater/reference solution.
  • Failure modes: Tank hum, long high-current heaters, unsafe service seam.
  • Effort: Medium; electrical interfaces plus mechanical relocation.
  • Decisive observation: Whole assembly fits and supply/noise response meets declared limits without added circuit changes.

K2 — Upper power assembly

  • Hypothesis: Repack existing upper power area, preserving current distribution as baseline.
  • Mechanisms: M1 + M3.
  • Benefit over baseline K2: Fewest new remote power interfaces.
  • Prerequisites: Resolve current carrier overruns and exact heavy-part support.
  • Failure modes: Upper crowding, heat, handle/cassette loading.
  • Effort: Low to medium; existing native consolidation still incomplete.
  • Decisive observation: Populated cassette fits with direct service and qualified heat/noise.

K3 — Floor plinth with reserved tank corridor

  • Hypothesis: Use a shaped lower power tray whose structure and connector face reserve the measured tank and extraction corridor.
  • Mechanisms: M3 + M4 + M7.
  • Benefit over baseline K2: May gain floor load support without pushing tank or service paths into unrecorded space.
  • Prerequisites: Exact floor/tank/speaker geometry and meaningful field test; no shielding credit from shape.
  • Failure modes: Custom-metal cost, loss of structural section, coupling remains unacceptable.
  • Effort: Medium to high; bounded custom bracket/tray study.
  • Decisive observation: Service mock-up and matched pickup meet gates at acceptable incremental cost.

K4 — More-complete lower power assembly

  • Hypothesis: Move raw reservoir and all four film-filter capacitors below, routing their distinct outputs/returns to the upper board.
  • Mechanisms: M1 + M3 + M5.
  • Benefit over baseline K2: Largest reduction in upper supply-body area.
  • Prerequisites: Exact long-node harness, insulation/charge state and local response qualification.
  • Failure modes: Extended sensitive loops, added contacts, tank hum, insufficient floor space.
  • Effort: Medium to high; interface validation and possible reallocation.
  • Decisive observation: Measured whole fit gain survives connector/support volume and response/noise checks.

K5 — Supported vertical power card in lower bay

  • Hypothesis: Place a structurally supported vertical power card beside floor-mounted magnetics, with accessible connector/service face.
  • Mechanisms: M3 + M7 + M8.
  • Benefit over baseline K2: Trades lower floor depth for height while keeping bulk loops within one lower module.
  • Prerequisites: Package orientation/body restraint, speaker envelope, ventilation and removal path.
  • Failure modes: Part/connector depth erases gain; top-heavy card loads or inaccessible screws.
  • Effort: Medium; parametric card/support study and interface review.
  • Decisive observation: Depth savings remain positive after actual supports/harness and all service/thermal gates.

K6 — Supported upper capacitor bank

  • Hypothesis: Retain upper supply architecture but use a supported vertical capacitor bank where depth trade is useful.
  • Mechanisms: M7 + M8.
  • Benefit over baseline K2: Potential local space gain without a new cabinet-height power harness.
  • Prerequisites: Exact capacitor retention, terminals, support and upper removal path.
  • Failure modes: Extra PCB/interconnects, retained charge, whole carrier still too large.
  • Effort: Medium; follows prior capacitor-fit plan.
  • Decisive observation: Improves whole upper cassette rather than detached bank dimensions.

Research-based information shortlist and protocols

Research retains K1 and K4 for comparison, K2 as diagnostic reference, defers K3/K5 until exact geometry and keeps K6 as a fallback replacement, not a third experiment. These are information priorities; no card has passed whole fit. Six frozen cards remain unchanged.

P1: compare K2/K1/K4 in one common populated geometry/inert service study, controlling exact parts, circuit values, tank plane, OT position and datums. Record every coupled wall/support/connector/cable/allocation change. Show collisions, whole envelopes, dimensional uncertainty and cover/screw/module/socket/reconnection sequence. Lightweight mock-ups cannot prove retention, thermal or electrical safety. Stop dependent work if unknown geometry/insulation/tool dimensions could reverse the result. Select P2 using the decision tree below. The passive K1/K4 filter comparison requires both lower options credible and a declared K4 whole-assembly gain beyond uncertainty; magnetic comparison may proceed for a sole credible lower option. P1 follows the shared desk-session checkpoint limit; physical setup requires its own effort cap and actual authorization.

P2 is conditional: compare K1/K4 local versus remote C12/C13/C25 network and R17/R35 placement with unchanged values, reservoir/C26/OT and deliberate returns. Existing10kΩ filters mean tiny cable resistance must not be presumed dominant. Freeze actual cable/contact route R/L/C, loads/steps, source impedance, bandwidth, allowable disturbance, instrument floor and uncertainty before testing. First use a reviewed current-limited low-voltage passive fixture with matched terminations. Document how terminations represent stage incremental loads/frequency range and omitted operating-voltage, active-load and nonlinear effects. If omissions could reverse the decision, the fixture is exploratory only. Use three repeated traces plus repeat reference; record load-end impedance/transfer/step response and shared-return disturbance. Stop for fixture ratings, unexpected voltage/charge, overload/current-limit or oscillation; never repair only one arm. Below-resolution results are ties/upper bounds; both must meet absolute requirements. No limits or resolvable predicted effect means hold P2.

If tank pickup becomes the dominant unknown, explicitly replace P2 with the bounded closed-source magnetic protocol from ../bottom-power-enclosure/magnetic-research.md; do not add a third experiment. De-energize and verify discharge before every movement. A passive low-voltage test proves no mains/HV/heater performance; a closed loaded mains field test proves no full-amplifier response. Full amp confirmation is later, after HD-002/bias/PE/discharge and other active-circuit gates pass, at both mains modes and relevant loads. No safe fixture or applicable authorization means plan only.

Pre-mortem decision tree and prerequisites

Before selecting a protocol, integration spends one bounded evidence-intake session (up to two hours) collecting existing drawings/measurements and producing a short missing-input register: mechanical owner—common floor/support/speaker/magnetic/connector envelopes; electrical owner—active fixture limits, retained charge, heater/bias interfaces and component temperature limits; sourcing owner—same-revision quantity-one incremental quotes; user—deferred drill readings, requested November26 for tomorrow (expected November27, pending actual receipt), with only specific additional measurements requested when needed. No study-budget limit authorizes guessed data or supplier contact. After that checkpoint, wait on dependent external inputs while progressing unrelated tasks.

Apply identical readiness categories to all six cards: geometrically bounded, electrically bounded, service sequence bounded, source/cost known, and decisive measurable benefit defined. K1/K4 are provisional user-question information priorities, not winners; if K3/K5/K6 are better bounded or resolve the real conflict, revise the shortlist explicitly before spending protocol effort. Unknown thermal/cost limits get named owners and block architecture acceptance, not a fictional pass.

Early tank-field risk screen is mandatory for every lower option: locate tank output and actual loaded magnetics, identify available feasible separation/headings, and record whether credible existing measured evidence addresses pickup. Absent that evidence, magnetic compatibility is a major unresolved gate; do no detailed lower fabrication commitment. P1 may remain an inert bounds/service study, not electrical feasibility approval.

After P1: (a) no lower option credible—stop lower study and replace, rather than add, with K2/K6 fallback if justified; (b) K1 credible but K4 fails or adds no useful gain—P2 may test K1 versus the upper reference for the decisive remaining magnetic uncertainty; (c) both credible with meaningful K4 gain—if magnetic uncertainty is still decisive, P2 is the matched magnetic test; only if that is already addressed and predicted filter effect is measurable and decision-relevant, choose the passive K1/K4 filter test. A filter test requires a prior sensitivity estimate tied to actual load and allowable disturbance, not merely availability of an audio interface. Two protocol slots total; further work is a new explicit checkpoint, not hidden expansion.

Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Cassette
A removable assembly carrying the electronics and its supports.
Envelope
The space occupied by a part or assembly, including stated allowances.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
Heater
The tube element that heats the cathode.
HV
High voltage; use the actual voltage and insulation requirement in each design check.
ICM
Interpretable Context Methodology; the project structure that records ownership, inputs, outputs and checks.
Impedance
Opposition to alternating current, including resistance and frequency-dependent effects; measured in ohms (Ω).
Lead
A component terminal that connects to the board.
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Magnetics
Transformers and inductors.
Mains
The building AC power supply.
MOQ
Minimum order quantity.
Node
An electrical connection shared by specified components.
PCB
Printed circuit board.
PE
Protective earth; the protective connection to exposed conductive parts.
Populated
With the components installed.
Preamp
Preamplifier; the stages that prepare the input signal for the power amplifier.
Qualification
Checks that show a design meets stated limits under stated conditions.
Reservoir
A capacitor bank that stores energy after rectification.
Socket
A replaceable connection that holds a tube or integrated circuit.
Standby
A switch state; it does not by itself prove the amplifier is de-energized.
Tank
The mechanical spring-reverb assembly.
K1
The candidate with the bulk and screen supply below and stage filters above.
K2
The upper power-assembly reference layout; it is not yet a proven fit.
K4
The candidate with all power-supply filters below.
Gate
A checkpoint that requires specified evidence before the next activity.
