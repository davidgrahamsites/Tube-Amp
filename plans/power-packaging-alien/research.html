P1 — one whole-system comparison, three states

Compare K2, K1 and K4 in one common populated model and inert service trial. This is one three-state system comparison, not a pure causal test of capacitor location. Keep exact compatible parts, circuit values, tank plane, output-transformer location, cabinet datums and unchanged audio/control parts common. Record all coupled changes: mounts, module walls, supports, routing, additional connectors, capacitor allocation, harness length and tank movement. K1 and K4 differ in both location and resulting lead/return geometry.

Start with the common dimension register; include installed speaker, actual magnetics, tube/socket envelopes, all populated boards, insulation-dependent exclusion regions, capacitor body restraints, plugs/latches/bends and actual driver/tool sweeps. Preserve unresolved dimensions explicitly. The Panasonic/CRC alternatives are separate part cases, not an opportunity to use the smaller body only in the favored card. Panasonic's maximum-body arithmetic and thermal neighbor spacing do not supply assembly insulation distances. Panasonic R9 specification, with the local geometry and mounting reports, supports that boundary.

Observable record: collision/clearance ledger with dimensional provenance and tolerance, occupied dimensions by assembly, required additional interfaces, and photographs/video of rear-cover removal/replacement, retaining-screw access, supported withdrawal, socket replacement and reconnection. Demonstrate the sequence without wire strain or electronics supported by removable rear wood. An inert model proves access only for its represented geometry; it cannot prove structural strength, thermal behavior or electrical safety. Mass/load-path qualification remains a separate hold if the mock-up is lightweight.

Stop/go: stop detailed dependent work when missing speaker, support, insulation or tool envelopes could reverse the result. Owner supplies the specific missing measurement/drawing; do not fill the gap with an estimate and score it as passed. Eliminate a state that violates a fixed requirement. Carry K1/K4 to P2 only if both remain mechanically credible and K4 buys a declared, measurable whole-assembly advantage beyond dimensional uncertainty. P2 — conditional paired filter/harness comparison

The cleanest allocation contrast is K1 versus K4, with C29/C30 and their rectifier charging loop unchanged below, C26/L1 unchanged, output transformer unchanged above, and the same four film references/values. Move the C12/C13/C25 filter network and associated R17/R35 ownership explicitly; do not change resistor values, add bypasses or merge returns. C13/C25 share a nominal node but remain separate physical references. The local interface report traces the native topology.

The mechanism is plausible, not already quantified: lead/return impedance affects the supply at its load. TI supports minimizing bypass loop area and considering parasitic inductance, but its source is a switching-converter layout article. Its preferred grounding scheme and MHz examples are not transferable amplifier rules. TI SSZTAL0. R17/R35 are already 10 kΩ; a tiny added cable resistance cannot be presumed to dominate their RC filtering. Assess the complete load-to-local/remote-capacitor loop, shared returns and coupling before paying for a bench comparison.

Entry sheet: freeze actual cable/contact parts, route lengths and geometry; DC resistance and bounded R/L/C; load-current envelope and step/slew; source impedance; bandwidth, gain and instrument uncertainty; settling time; fixture voltage/current limits; and absolute permitted rail disturbance/noise plus allowable difference. Derive these from the actual circuit/load requirement. If numeric limits or a resolvable predicted effect are absent, P2 remains a drafted hold, not an executable pass/fail claim.

Low-voltage stage: use an independently reviewed closed, current-limited low-voltage passive fixture with the actual filter parts and representative terminations. Compare identical injection/load conditions at the load-side supply/return pair. Record transfer/impedance and step response, simultaneous shared-return disturbance where relevant, open/dummy fixture floor, and three repeated traces followed by a repeat reference. Preserve raw traces, calibration, settings and exact connection diagram. Change connections only de-energized after charge verification. This tests passive harness/filter behavior within its stimulus range; it does not reproduce high-voltage bias, tube loading, actual transformer ripple, insulation stress, heater behavior or complete amplifier noise/stability.

Decision: if the difference is below combined repeatability/uncertainty, record a tie or upper bound. A relatively better trace is not a pass when both violate the absolute requirement. Stop on oscillation, current-limit operation, instrument overload, unexpected charge/voltage, wrong return or any fixture rating violation; do not silently add corrective parts to one arm. Additional circuit fixes require a new matched baseline.

Limits that must survive synthesis

  • Reject K3/K5 as a purported single-variable pair: structure, board orientation and service geometry change together. Reject lead-suspended capacitors, unspecified sideways body bending, shared chassis operating return, rear-cover structural support and a generic header treated as a qualified HV service seam.
  • A bonded metal box is not measured 50/60 Hz tank shielding. Jensen distinguishes capacitive Faraday screening from magnetic shielding. Jensen manufacturer FAQ. Tank pickup may be more decisive than remote-filter parasitics; if it becomes the dominant uncertainty, replace P2 at the checkpoint with the existing magnetic report's bounded placement protocol rather than adding a third experiment.
  • A source/tank field test can precede a complete amplifier only when the actual energized source assembly itself is enclosed, fused, bonded, loaded and reviewed, with protected low-voltage sensing. It remains a mains experiment even if the measured output is millivolts. Keep tank horizontal/open-down, actual loads/headings and recovery gain fixed; use power-off repositioning, background/reference repeats and both actual 120 V/60 Hz and 220 V/50 Hz conditions. An unloaded or waveform-unrepresentative source gives only a preliminary screen.
  • A passive low-voltage fixture has no mains-mode qualification. A closed field fixture has no full-amplifier qualification. Neither inherits or resolves the unfinished amplifier's heater/reference fault. That fault must be resolved when its affected circuits are active; bias loss with B+ present, insulation/PE, retained-charge paths and load ratings must likewise be resolved for each energized fixture's actual scope.
  • Full-amplifier confirmation later needs both mains modes, relevant idle/load conditions, dry/wet noise, rated output load, response/stability and covers-on thermal evidence. Do not imply it occurs inside the present two-protocol budget or that a Mac audio input may connect directly to HV nodes.

Vocabulary key

Terms used in this report. See the full key for related terms.

Bias
A steady voltage or current that sets a device operating point.
Capacitor
A component that stores charge and electrical energy.
Chassis
The metal structure that supports and encloses the electronics.
Clearance
The shortest distance through air between conductive parts.
DC
Direct current; current that flows in one direction.
Envelope
The space occupied by a part or assembly, including stated allowances.
Harness
A grouped set of wires, contacts and connectors.
Heater
The tube element that heats the cathode.
HV
High voltage; use the actual voltage and insulation requirement in each design check.
Impedance
Opposition to alternating current, including resistance and frequency-dependent effects; measured in ohms (Ω).
Lead
A component terminal that connects to the board.
Load
The circuit or device that takes current from a source.
Magnetics
Transformers and inductors.
Mains
The building AC power supply.
Node
An electrical connection shared by specified components.
Nominal
The stated reference value before tolerance and operating variation.
PE
Protective earth; the protective connection to exposed conductive parts.
Populated
With the components installed.
Qualification
Checks that show a design meets stated limits under stated conditions.
Rectifier
A circuit that changes alternating current into current of one polarity.
Ripple
Periodic supply variation remaining after rectification and filtering.
Socket
A replaceable connection that holds a tube or integrated circuit.
Tank
The mechanical spring-reverb assembly.
Tolerance
The permitted variation from a specified value.
Transformer
A magnetic device that transfers energy between windings.
K1
The candidate with the bulk and screen supply below and stage filters above.
K2
The upper power-assembly reference layout; it is not yet a proven fit.
K4
The candidate with all power-supply filters below.
