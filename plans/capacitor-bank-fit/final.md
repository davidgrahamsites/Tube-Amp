# Capacitor-bank fit improvement plan

## Recommended direction
Start with a dedicated capacitor area on the revised power PCB, comparing a compact 2×2 group and a single compact row. Compare a supported vertical daughterboard if A fails a hard constraint or a bounded comparison demonstrates a material improvement in whole-cassette fit, retention or service that justifies its extra hardware and wiring. Consider an upper horizontal daughterboard only if it genuinely shares a cool, serviceable area. Option A is the dedicated power-PCB capacitor area, B the supported upper horizontal daughterboard, and C the supported vertical daughterboard. This is a prioritized investigation, not a selected layout or fabrication release.

Keep C12/C13/C25/C26 in the power-supply functional block. Preserve the intended three-board architecture where practical; a daughterboard adds a physical board even though functional ownership is unchanged. Current native CAD still has four incomplete boards. No unsupported lead-mounted elevation, cabinet alteration or audio redesign is included. The wooden rear cover remains mechanically independent of all electronics.

## Evidence that controls the comparison
[Interface research](interface-research.md) verifies C12: B_EARLY/GND; C13 and C25: B_PRE/GND; C26: B_SCREEN/GND, each 50 uF. Preserve all four references and deliberate physical returns; do not combine capacitors or use metal supports as returns. C29/C30 are the separate raw reservoir. Keep its charging loop, permanent PE and separate GB1 bond intact.

[Package and mounting research](mounting-research.md) leaves both candidates unselected. Panasonic EZPV60506MTC has a maximum 58×26×41 mm body and 4±2 mm exposed leads; CRC FC6136 is 58.5×36×51 mm with 4±1 mm leads. On nominal 1.6 mm PCB, minimum seated protrusion is only 0.4/1.4 mm before thickness tolerance and solder requirements. Neither document qualifies sideways/inverted mounting or retention hardware. Rotating an entire board is a conditional study, not permission to bend bodies on short leads.

Panasonic's [R9 specification](https://industrial.panasonic.com/content/data/CP/PDF/ezpv_je.pdf) recommends 10 mm neighbor spacing for heating; this is not HV clearance. Current/self-heating derate above 70°C and voltage above 85°C. Its 19.1 A at 70°C/10 kHz does not qualify amplifier ripple; startup/standby pulse compatibility also remains open. CRC current conditions and conflicting lifetime evidence need resolution. Do not commit detailed placement before candidate electrical and mounting viability is screened.

[Independent geometry research](geometry-research.md) gives these local body-plus-nominal-PCB extents, excluding supports, solder tails, board margins, insulation, connectors and extraction space:

| Candidate arrangement | Width × depth × height, mm |
|---|---|
| Panasonic horizontal 2×2 | 126 × 62 × 42.6 |
| Panasonic horizontal compact row | 134 × 58 × 42.6 |
| Panasonic vertical 2×2 | 126 × 42.6 × 62 |
| Panasonic vertical compact row | 134 × 42.6 × 58 |

The 10 mm gap is sourced for Panasonic and only a comparison assumption for CRC. These are not finished PCB sizes. The vertical Panasonic grouping trades 19.4 mm local depth for height; it does not prove removal of the whole carrier's 20 mm depth overrun. Existing diagnostic carriers also overrun width by 41 mm. Removing capacitors cannot by itself repair Main/Reverb width or shrink Power's remaining components/routing. Use candidate-specific heights, not the register's historical 41 mm scalar for CRC.

## Execution, in dependency order
1. **Freeze evidence and whole-cassette constraints — integration; one session.** Hash native inputs and record dimensions as measured, manufacturer, assumed or unknown in the existing mechanical interface. Cabinet readings 467 mm width, 160 mm depth and 383 mm height are not yet a verified usable cuboid; control opening is 307–308 by approximately 53 mm. Include board/control consolidation, speaker, transformers, tubes, supports, rear cover and withdrawal paths. Classify each missing input as blocking ranking, blocking fabrication, or permitting bounded preliminary work. **Done:** sourced constraint table, common frames where known, visible 41/20 mm baseline deficits and dependency list; unknown volume is not free space.
2. **Screen exact parts and interfaces — component/electrical owner; one to two sessions, external evidence may extend this.** Map each reference to candidate value, nodes and maximum electrical stress under both mains modes. Check voltage, ripple spectrum, pulse, temperature/lifetime, terminal grouping, insertion and body retention. Define the insulation basis using actual node voltages/transients, environment, material, PE relationships and tolerances; no guessed numeric gaps. Include solder, terminals, underside copper, fasteners and brackets. **Done:** viability table with sourced pass/fail/unknown and owner for every open gate. If neither part is viable, return to exact-part selection before detailed CAD.
3. **Model A, then conditional C/B — mechanical integration; one to two sessions after viable inputs.** Repack the complete power area, not a detached attractive bank. Include all other board functions and retain whole-cassette deficits. Use common parameters for editable FreeCAD and dimensioned views. Add actual body restraint and support load paths; board standoffs alone do not qualify capacitor retention. Unknown support/separation values stay explicit and block feasibility ranking. **Done:** comparable populated candidates with total envelope, local versus overall gains, hardware count, estimated cost basis, thermal exclusions and service paths. Reject alternatives whose support/wiring erases their gain.
4. **Prove electrical and service seams — electrical/mechanical integration; one session plus physical evidence.** Trace recharge/load return loops; assess added conductor length/common impedance and keep noisy paths away from audio. For a daughterboard, define the exact connector and pin map; qualify operating/fault voltage and current, insulation, touch protection in relevant unmated states, insulated wiring, strain relief, retention and prevention of incorrect reconnection; define safe disconnect/reconnect order. Review charged, off, standby-open, partly disconnected and removed states. Existing R506/R507 provide a downstream discharge path, but an unplugged bank may lose it. Establish residual-voltage/time criteria, retained discharge paths and measurement points; verify worst-case decay and physical procedure before passing the service gate. Additional bleeders are separate circuit changes. **Done:** no failed or unknown electrical/service gate counted as a pass.
5. **Verify removal, heat and tools; choose — integration with competent physical builder; one to two sessions plus bench time.** Make an inert full-size mock-up before releasing mechanical dimensions, and then test the actual supported assembly. Demonstrate rear-cover removal/replacement without releasing electronics or disconnecting wiring; retaining screws; cassette withdrawal; bank/PCB/tube replacement; reverse assembly. Distinguish drilling/deburring bare metal from screwdriving at each assembly stage. Keep direct retaining-screw access and bench PCB access; extensions cannot waive access. Check thermal conditions with covers and real loads, and retention under handling/vibration. Inspect CAD/export bounds/interference and actual harness/tool sweeps. **Done:** hard constraints pass before cost/simplicity ranking; prefer A if equivalent. No weighted score overrides a failure. After applicable interface gates pass, prepare provisional native CAD and prototype-only drawings to obtain physical evidence. Native changes require net preservation, ERC/DRC and renewed fit checks. Physical ordering/fabrication needs its own actual authorization; this plan does not grant it. Final production release follows populated prototype qualification.
6. **Close decisions and preserve evidence — integration and Luna journal; one short session.** Record selected option or conditional shortlist, failed options and evidence in ICM, update the journal, run scoped checks, rebuild catalogs, and commit/push scoped work under existing authorization. **Done:** reproducible files, status and Git receipt; an incomplete shortlist must say fit remains unresolved and carry the next action rather than claiming build readiness.

## Dependency ownership and closure
Integration owns cabinet datums, full-assembly model and constraint closure; component/electrical integration owns exact capacitor/magnetics/speaker selection, insulation and discharge qualification; mechanical integration owns support geometry and fastener paths. Review open items at the next engineering checkpoint and after each exact-part selection, before detailed dependent placement. The user supplies the deferred drill measurements tomorrow: 46 mm chuck is known; bit reach and full body are not. Do not repeatedly request them today. The competent builder owns later inert fit, thermal, vibration and discharge measurements. Record actual completion dates and evidence; missing supplier/physical inputs remain explicit holds, not assumed deadlines met.

Completion of this review means a rigorous executable plan and bounded comparison. Completion of capacitor fit requires the chosen populated assembly and service checks. Fabrication remains held until exact parts, insulation, hardware, full tool envelope and physical checks support it.
