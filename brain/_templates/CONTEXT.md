# Record templates
One job: define reusable record shapes.

## Inputs
[Brain schema](../CONTEXT.md).

## Process
Copy the matching template into `../records/` and replace all placeholders. Keep supplier choices and project results out of templates.

## Outputs
Decision, component, validation and delegated-task templates. Task cards are planning artifacts rather than indexed evidence records; place them with the corresponding engineering task.

## Human check
Filled records must explain what is known, how it is known and what remains unchecked.
