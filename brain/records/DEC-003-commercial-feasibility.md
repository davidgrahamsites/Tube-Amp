# Evaluate sale before committing to four repeat units

## Question and authority
The user asked what five units might sell for and whether buyers would exist. This asks for commercial analysis; it does not establish demand or authorize sales/posts/payments.

## Current direction
Build one validated demonstrator, test a costed price and support proposition with relevant Shanghai players, then decide whether repeat production is justified. Keep cash surplus distinct from owner-labor-inclusive profit. Current plan versions carry the cost and price-test calculations.

## Evidence and limits
Primary Chinese branded-retailer listings exist but accessible current comparable RMB prices were not verified. [Mable's handwired 5E3 with cabinet](https://mableaudio.com/en/productview.asp?sid=6) is a USD price anchor explicitly excluding speaker; it is not the recovered circuit.

[CNCA 2022 removal](https://www.cnca.gov.cn/zwxx/gg/zjgg/art/2022/art_08ab3d8ec62842959fe3c602db6113b5.html) supports classification review rather than assuming CCC applies. [GB4943.1-2022 official entry](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=862145EBF14F5CC3771FC64CE94A48DF) and [Product Quality Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zfjcj/tzgg/art/2023/art_579118cd202a45fba28b7edfd9f6fd72.html) inform the separate competent commercial review. Safety, EMC, RoHS, labeling, warranty/returns and costs remain scoped-review items.

## Next check
Resolve the source, target power/BOM, prototype performance, conformity cost and meaningful interest at a viable price. No completed sales evidence or actual buyer commitments have been obtained.
