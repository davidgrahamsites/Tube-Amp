# Loaded recovery and fault limits

CB-006 · The PA340CC and OPA134PA models drove the CB-005 overload target at 135.54 V peak and 0.292% THD. A sagged supply failed at 1.367% THD. Qualify the protected supply, startup mute, serviceable carrier and heat path before placement. [Engineering record](../../engineering/whole-amp/effects-loop/common-buffer/apex-return.html).
