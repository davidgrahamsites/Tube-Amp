# Composite return under modeled amplifier load

[CB-005 engineering evidence](../../engineering/whole-amp/effects-loop/common-buffer/composite-review.html#integrated-load) joins the nominal composite recovery to the source-derived downstream load. The retained 135 V peak overload exceeds the 1% distortion screen at 20 kHz with zero added wiring capacitance. Use the independent port-current calculation and the integrated transient results before selecting devices or assigning connector voltage domains. Hardware and populated placement remain open.
