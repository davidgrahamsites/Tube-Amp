# One working prototype before production

## Question and scope
Which milestone should govern immediate electrical, physical and sourcing work?

## Options and tradeoffs
The existing plan covers five units and commercial feasibility. A one-unit prototype first establishes actual fit and function before repeat-build or sales commitments; quantity-one costs must be established independently.

## Evidence
The user stated in this Codex chat on October 4, 2026: “The first plan is to a working prototype that will fit in the box.” The requirement is recorded in the [canonical handoff](../../Renegade_Codex_Handoff.md); work packages are in the [active plan](../../plans/renegade-working-prototype/plan.md).

## Decision and authority
User-directed scope: first complete one functioning integrated combo in the chosen cabinet. Cabinet geometry and source circuit recovery are early parallel inputs. Preserve the previous production/sales plan as a future reference. This changes scope priority, not electrical requirements or the need for review before energizing.

## Open questions and next check
The original schematic is received and initially inspected; reconcile its ambiguities and obtain real shell/chassis dimensions. Review a component-envelope fit layout before committing board or panel geometry. Power target and quantity-one budget remain open. Main controls may face upward on the top or forward on the front, per [DEC-002](DEC-002-combo-shanghai-automation.md); the final layout remains to be chosen. Completion requires measured operation of the assembled combo, not only file generation or a CAD fit.
