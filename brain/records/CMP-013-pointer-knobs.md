# Pointer knob and potentiometer interface

User supplied a black sculpted pointer knob with a white index as the preferred visual direction. Preferred sourcing candidate is Mable MAKP0437S, nominal 26 × 16.5 mm, listed USD1.65 each at ten. Catalog is not a stock confirmation or domestic quote.

Supplier photo shows a metal insert. Supplier drawing offers 6 and 6.4 mm shaft holes. Genuine Sato K-2195-S manufacturer data covers a 6 mm shaft and two retaining screws; do not assume the Mable 6.4 mm variant is genuine Sato or inherits all manufacturer specifications. Exact supplier identity, bore variant, pot shaft/bushing and panel fit remain release dependencies.

See [sourcing dossier](../../engineering/component-selection/knobs-and-controls.md) for primary links, dimensions, price, control quantities and release conditions. Keep the ten faceplate knobs distinct from the two internal trimmers. REVERB adds wet to constant dry.

At 32 mm centers, ten 26 mm knobs need 314 mm end-to-end before margins. Do not freeze a single row from the published 312 mm cabinet opening; usable chassis geometry is still unverified.
