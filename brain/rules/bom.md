# Bill of materials

Scope: reusable workflow guidance, not evidence of verified circuitry.

## BOM-001
Make reference designators traceable to schematic items. Distinguish fitted parts, do-not-fit options, chassis hardware and wiring; make quantities intentional.

## BOM-002
Use fields for reference designators, quantity, function/value, required ratings, manufacturer, exact part number, package/mounting, footprint, supplier, alternatives, evidence record, status and procurement notes.

## BOM-003
Keep engineering requirements separate from supplier prices and availability. Capture quote date, currency, quantity and MOQ.

## BOM-004
Reconcile the BOM with the actual schematic revision and release package. Document gaps and substitutions instead of guessing.

## Basis and exceptions
Seeded from [the handoff and discussion](../sources.md) and this requested engineering workflow. Record supporting evidence and exceptions as work proceeds. Project-specific values and results belong in [records](../records/CONTEXT.md).
