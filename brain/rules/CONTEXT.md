# Reusable engineering rules
One job: hold guidance usable across KiCad projects.

## Inputs
[Sources](../sources.md), [project evidence records](../records/CONTEXT.md), and documented experience.

## Process
Maintain the relevant domain file. Use stable rule IDs, state scope and rationale, and link supporting evidence. Supplier choices, numeric design values and current outcomes belong in records.

## Outputs
Rule-sets for schematic capture, BOM, component selection, PCB and manufacture.

## Human check
Check whether guidance generalizes beyond one circuit; retain exceptions and distinguish workflow practice from verified electrical claims.
