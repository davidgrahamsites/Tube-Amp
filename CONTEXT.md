## Process
Start with [the project-wide material map](brain/catalog/WORKSPACE.md). It links current artifacts, historical revisions, received hardware, selected parts and pending work. Use [the feedback workflow](brain/rules/feedback.md) to capture new corrections and decisions as they arrive. The [feedback view](brain/catalog/FEEDBACK.md) is generated from the editable event ledger.

Use [the brain contract](brain/CONTEXT.md) to retrieve and capture knowledge during each task. Read the relevant guidance and records, then consult their evidence. Preserve the imported source files.

Follow the [active prototype plan](plans/renegade-working-prototype/plan.md): first make one functioning combo fit the chosen box. The five-unit production and sales plan is a later reference.

## Outputs
Engineering artifacts remain in project folders. Reusable knowledge and evidence records live in `brain/`; project inventory remains in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.

The complete visible-file inventory and ownership map live in `brain/catalog/`. Original files and historical CAD paths are preserved; historical folders are reference snapshots, not templates for new active work. Stable catalog factories live in `brain/tools/`, editable registers in `brain/materials/` and `brain/feedback/`, generated views in `brain/catalog/`.

Supporting equipment designs start at [engineering/CONTEXT.md](engineering/CONTEXT.md), including the [EL34 / 12AX7 benchtop matcher](engineering/tube-matcher/CONTEXT.md).

## Human check
Review unresolved electrical decisions and evidence before fabrication or energizing a build. Ordinary documentation and reversible work can continue autonomously.
